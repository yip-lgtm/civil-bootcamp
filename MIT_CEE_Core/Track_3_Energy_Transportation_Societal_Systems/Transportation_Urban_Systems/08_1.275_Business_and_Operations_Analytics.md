## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **Process 視為 input → transformation → output**  
   SIPOC model; each step has capability, variability, cost。
2. **Variability 係 system performance 嘅 enemy**  
   Variability reduction (Six Sigma) improves quality, cost, time。
3. **Inventory 對沖 variability, 但 hidden cost**  
   Trade-off: holding cost vs stockout cost。
4. **Bottleneck theory (TOC) 聚焦 system 嘅 1-2 constraints**  
   Optimizing non-bottlenecks 唔增加 throughput。
5. **Queueing theory 對服務系統**  
   M/M/1, M/M/c — Little's law, utilization vs wait time。

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **Lean vs Six Sigma vs Theory of Constraints**  
   - Lean: Waste elimination, flow.  
   - Six Sigma: Variability reduction, statistical.  
   - TOC: Constraint focus, throughput.

2. **Push vs pull production**  
   - Push (MRP): Forecast-driven, inventory.  
   - Pull (JIT, Kanban): Demand-driven, lean.

3. **Centralized vs decentralized operations**  
   - Centralized: Economies of scale, slower.  
   - Decentralized: Resilience, redundancy.
