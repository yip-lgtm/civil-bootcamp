# Track 3 — Energy, Transportation & Societal Systems (Systems)
**MIT CEE Core | 54–66 Units (CORE) + 48–60 Units (REs)**

---

The **Systems Engineering** track (page title: "Energy, Transportation, and
Societal Systems Track") focuses on mathematical methods — network science,
optimization, control theory, and data analytics — applied to urban systems
like transportation, supply chains, energy, water, and societal networks.

The MIT page shows two main **focus** sub-areas within Systems:
- **Transportation focus**
- **Data analytics focus**
