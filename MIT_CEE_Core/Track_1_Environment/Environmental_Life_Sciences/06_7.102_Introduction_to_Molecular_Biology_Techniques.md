## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **Central dogma drives experimental design**  
   DNA → RNA → protein; each step has dedicated techniques。
2. **PCR 係 molecular biology 嘅 amplifier**  
   30 cycles = 10⁹ amplification; revolutionised biology。
3. **Gel electrophoresis separates by size/charge**  
   Agarose for DNA, SDS-PAGE for proteins。
4. **Cloning 係 DNA 嘅 recombinant engineering**  
   Restriction enzymes + ligation + transformation。
5. **Sequencing 對 identity 確定**  
   Sanger (single) vs NGS (population) vs long-read (PacBio, Nanopore)。

---

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **Wet-lab vs in silico**  
   - Wet: Reality, slow.  
   - In silico: Fast, hypothetical.

2. **Sanger vs NGS**  
   - Sanger: Gold standard single-read.  
   - NGS: Population, throughput.

3. **CRISPR vs traditional genome editing**  
   - CRISPR: Fast, cheap, specific.  
   - Traditional (TALEN, ZFN): Older, less accessible.

---

## 問題 3：生成 10 個能區分深度理解與死背知識的問題

1. 給定一個 plasmid + insert, 設計 cloning strategy (restriction sites, ligation)。
2. 解釋 PCR 嘅 exponential amplification kinetics 同 primer design 嘅 trade-offs。
3. 為什麼 gel electrophoresis 嘅 band intensity 唔係絕對 quantitation？
4. 給定一個 sequencing read, 用 BLAST identify 來源 organism。
5. 解釋「CRISPR-Cas9」嘅 mechanism 同 sgRNA design。
6. 為什麼「NGS library prep」對 sequencing quality 至關重要？
7. 解釋「qPCR」對 gene expression quantitation 嘅 Ct method。
8. 給定一個 protein, 設計 Western blot antibody + protocol。
9. 為什麼「sterile technique」對微生物学 experiments 重要？
10. 設計一個 end-to-end molecular biology project: DNA extraction → PCR → sequencing → analysis。
