# Track 1 — Environment
**MIT CEE Core | 54–66 Units (CORE) + 48–60 Units (REs)**

---

The Environment Track focuses on the science, engineering, and management of
natural and engineered environmental systems. It has two sub-areas:

## Sub-areas

| Sub-area | Focus | Courses |
|---|---|---|
| [Environmental life sciences](./Environmental_Life_Sciences/) | Microbiology, public health, environmental biology | 2 courses |
| [Fluids and transport engineering](./Fluids_Transport_Engineering/) | Hydrology, transport, chemistry, fieldwork | 6 courses |

## Self-study path

1. **Year 3:** Take 1.061 (Transport Processes) — it's the foundation
2. **Year 3:** Pair with 1.080 (Chemistry) for the chemistry track
3. **Year 3:** Pair with 1.018/1.070 (Ecology/Hydrology) for the hydrology track
4. **Year 3:** Take 1.106 + 1.107 labs alongside theory
5. **Summer between Y3/Y4:** Apply for TREX (1.091) — field experience
6. **Year 4:** 1.063 + 1.081 for the public-health angle
7. **Post-grad:** M.Eng in Environmental Engineering
