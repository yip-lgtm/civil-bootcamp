# MIT_CEE_Core — Core Coursework (Choose One Track)
**MIT Course 1-ENG | 54–66 Units (CORE) + 48–60 Units (REs)**

---

The Core is the specialization phase of the CEE undergraduate program. Every
student **chooses one** of three Core Subject Areas. Each Track has:

- **54–66 Units Core Coursework (CORE):** the foundational subjects of the area
- **48–60 Units Restricted Electives (REs):** deeper specialization within the area

## Choose ONE

| # | Track | Sub-areas |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Environment](./Track_1_Environment/00_INDEX.md) | Environmental life sciences · Fluids and transport engineering |
| 2 | [Mechanics & Materials](./Track_2_Mechanics_Materials/00_INDEX.md) | Structural Design · Materials |
| 3 | [Energy, Transportation & Societal Systems](./Track_3_Energy_Transportation_Societal_Systems/00_INDEX.md) | Transportation and Urban Systems · Energy Systems |

## Self-study path

1. **Year 2:** Sample one course from each Track to feel the difference
2. **End of Year 2:** Declare a Track
3. **Year 3+:** Complete CORE + REs in the chosen Track
4. **Year 4:** Capstone design / thesis
5. **Post-grad:** [MEng SMD](../MIT_CEE_MEng_SMD/00_INDEX.md) is the natural continuation
   for the Mechanics & Materials track
