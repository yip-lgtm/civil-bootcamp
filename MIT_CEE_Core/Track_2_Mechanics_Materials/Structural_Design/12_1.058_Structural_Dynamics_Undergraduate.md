## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **SDOF 係 building block**  
   Multi-DOF = coupled SDOFs; SDOF intuition transfers。
2. **Free vibration 揭示 system dynamics**  
   Natural frequency, damping, mode shape — these are properties, not responses。
3. **Forced vibration 嘅 resonance 係 design driver**  
   Operating frequency ≈ natural frequency = disaster。
4. **Damping 係 small but critical**  
   5% damping changes peak response 10×。
5. **Modal analysis 解耦**  
   Transform physical coords → modal coords → uncoupled SDOFs。

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **Time-domain vs frequency-domain analysis**  
   - Time: Transient, nonlinear.  
   - Frequency: Steady-state, linear.

2. **Linear vs nonlinear dynamics**  
   - Linear: Modal, fast.  
   - Nonlinear (large deflection, contact): Realistic, slow.

3. **Deterministic vs stochastic (random vibration)**  
   - Deterministic: Single excitation.  
   - Stochastic: PSD-driven, statistics of response.
