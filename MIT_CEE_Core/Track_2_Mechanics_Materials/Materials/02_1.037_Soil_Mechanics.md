## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **土是三相材料（固、液、氣），有效應力才是真正的應力**  
   Terzaghi’s effective stress principle is the single most important idea in soil mechanics.

2. **強度與剛度強烈依賴應力歷史與路徑**  
   OCR, consolidation history, and drainage conditions dominate behaviour more than “soil type” alone.

3. **破壞是局部化過程（剪帶）**  
   Peak strength is not a material constant; it depends on the formation of shear bands and progressive failure.

4. **滲流與力學必須耦合**  
   Consolidation, liquefaction, and seepage-induced instability are hydro-mechanical problems.

5. **現場變異與不確定性是第一階效應**  
   Spatial variability and limited site investigation data make probabilistic thinking essential.

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **經驗方法（規範、圖表） vs 進階本構模型（Cam-Clay, hypoplasticity）**  
   - Empirical: Fast, calibrated to local experience, code-accepted.  
   - Advanced constitutive: Captures stress-path dependence and can extrapolate better.

2. **總應力分析 vs 有效應力分析**  
   - Total stress: Simpler for undrained short-term problems.  
   - Effective stress: Fundamentally correct and necessary for long-term and cyclic loading.

3. **確定性安全係數 vs 可靠度 / 風險基礎設計**  
   - Factor of safety: Simple communication and legal clarity.  
   - Reliability: Quantifies actual risk and optimizes investigation effort.
