## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **材料退化是時間、環境與使用的函數**  
   Heritage materials have already undergone decades or centuries of aging; understanding that history is essential.

2. **最小干預原則**  
   Any intervention must be reversible or at least re-treatable; the original material is the primary document.

3. **多學科是唯一可行的方法**  
   Materials science, chemistry, history, architecture, and conservation ethics must work together.

4. **診斷先於治療**  
   Characterization of current state and degradation mechanisms must precede any treatment decision.

5. **價值不只是物質的**  
   Cultural, historical, and social values constrain technical options more tightly than in new construction.

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **保存原真性 vs 恢復可使用性**  
   - Authenticity: Preserve as found, even if fragile.  
   - Usability: Allow continued or renewed function, accepting some material change.

2. **傳統工藝 vs 現代材料與技術**  
   - Traditional: Compatible, reversible, historically appropriate.  
   - Modern: Can offer superior performance and longer service life.

3. **預防性保存 vs 積極修復**  
   - Preventive: Control environment, reduce future damage.  
   - Interventive: Necessary when damage has already progressed.
