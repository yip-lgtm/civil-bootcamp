# MIT_CEE_GIRs — General Institute Requirements
**MIT Course 1-ENG | 17 Subjects | Foundation for All CEE Tracks**

---

The General Institute Requirements (GIRs) are the **17 subjects every MIT undergraduate
must complete**, regardless of major. They cover science, mathematics, humanities,
arts, and social sciences. For CEE students, the GIRs form the quantitative foundation
for all upper-division structural, environmental, and systems courses.

**Source:** [MIT GIRs](https://catalog.mit.edu/mit/undergraduate-education/general-institute-requirements/);
[MIT Catalog — Science Requirement](https://catalog.mit.edu/mit/undergraduate-education/general-institute-requirements/general-institute-requirements_sciencerequirementtext.pdf)

## Status

This folder is a **cross-reference index** — GIRs are administered by other MIT departments
(Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics). The CEE program references them in its degree
requirements but the subjects themselves belong to their home departments.

**MIT OCW resources** for GIR preparation:
- [MIT OCW 8.01 Physics I](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-01sc-calculus-with-precalculus-fall-2010/)

## Self-study Path for CEE Students

1. **Year 1 Fall:** 18.01 (Calculus I) + 8.01 (Physics I) + 3.091 or 5.111 (Chemistry)
2. **Year 1 Spring:** 18.02 (Calculus II) + 8.02 (Physics II) + Biology (7.01x)
3. **Year 2:** 18.03 (Differential Equations) → enables 1.036, 1.063
4. **Year 2:** 18.06 (Linear Algebra) → enables 1.022, 1.010A
5. **Year 2:** 6.0001 (Python) → enables 1.000, 1.021
6. **Years 1–2:** Complete REST subject (CEE-specific options available: 1.063, 1.009)

## Note on "ENGG5401–ENGG5405"

The civil-bootcamp repo also contains files labeled ENGG5401–ENGG5405.
These are **placeholder stubs** from the original course generation system and do not
correspond to actual MIT CEE course numbers. The legitimate MIT CEE undergraduate catalog
begins with Course 1 subjects (1.001, 1.010A, etc.) and graduate subjects (1.50x, 1.54x,
1.56x, 1.57x, 1.58x, 1.60x, etc.).

For actual MIT CEE course listings, consult:
- [MIT Catalog — CEE Subjects](https://catalog.mit.edu/subjects/1/)
- [MIT CEE Undergraduate](https://cee.mit.edu/education/undergraduate/)
