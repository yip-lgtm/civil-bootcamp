# MIT_CEE_GIRs — General Institute Requirements
**MIT Course 1-ENG | 17 Subjects**

---

The General Institute Requirements (GIRs) are the 17 subjects every MIT undergraduate
must complete, regardless of major. They cover science, mathematics, humanities,
arts, and social sciences.

**Source:** [MIT GIRs](https://catalog.mit.edu/mit/undergraduate-education/general-institute-requirements/)

## Categories

| Category | Subjects | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Science core** | Physics I & II, Chemistry, Biology | 12 units |
| **Mathematics** | Calculus I & II (18.01, 18.02) | 24 units |
| **Humanities** | 8 subjects distributed across departments | 24 units |
| **Arts** | 1 subject | variable units |
| **REST** | 1 subject in Restricted Elective Science & Tech | variable units |
| **Lab** | 1 subject | 12 units |
| **Communication** | 2 subjects (CI-H, CI-HW) | variable units |

## Status

This folder is a **placeholder** — GIRs are not CEE-specific. The CEE program
references GIRs in its degree requirements but the subjects themselves are
administered by other MIT departments.

See [MIT GIRs catalog](https://catalog.mit.edu/mit/undergraduate-education/general-institute-requirements/)
for the canonical list.

---

**Self-study path:** GIRs are typically completed in Years 1–2 of the MIT
undergraduate program. They form the foundation for all subsequent CEE coursework.
