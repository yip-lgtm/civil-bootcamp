## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **Modern architecture 係 1920s–1970s 嘅 specific movement**  
   Not all "modern-looking" is "Modern Movement" — distinction matters.
2. **技術 + 意識形態 = 建築**  
   Steel, concrete, elevator + Le Corbusier, Bauhaus, CIAM = Modern.
3. **Manifestos > buildings**  
   Modern architects 寫 嘅 manifestos 同 buildings 一樣重要。
4. **Tabula rasa vs 連續性**  
   Modernism 主張斷裂, 但每個 project 都喺 context 入面。
5. **Modernism 嘅反動 = Postmodernism**  
   Venturi, Graves, Eisenman — 1960s reaction 同 Modernism 互相定義。

---

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **Modernism 嘅 legacy**  
   - Pro: Functionalism, mass production, social progress.  
   - Anti: Cultural homogenization, urban renewal failures.

2. **Pioneers vs second-generation moderns**  
   - Pioneers (Wright, Gropius, Le Corbusier): Original vision.  
   - Second-gen (Mies, Aalto): Refinement, regional adaptation.

3. **High Modernism vs Critical Regionalism**  
   - High Modernism: Universal, international.  
   - Critical Regionalism (Frampton): Place-based, tectonic.
