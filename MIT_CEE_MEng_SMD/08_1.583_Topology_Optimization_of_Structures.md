## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **Topology = 0/1 material distribution**  
   Each element is either solid (1) or void (0); the field of those values is the topology.
2. **Compliance minimization 係最 fundamental 嘅 TO problem**  
   Minimize strain energy subject to volume constraint; yields optimal load paths.
3. **Penalization (SIMP) forces discrete solutions**  
   Without penalization, intermediate densities dominate; p ≥ 3 enforces near-0/1.
4. **Mesh dependency 係 main numerical issue**  
   Without filtering, results form checkerboards; need density/mesh filters.
5. **Symmetry-breaking from boundary conditions**  
   Loading and supports break symmetry; optimal topology often asymmetric.

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **SIMP vs Level-Set vs Phase-Field**  
   - SIMP: Fast, density-based, gray-region issue.  
   - Level-set: Sharp boundaries, harder to parameterize.  
   - Phase-field: Physics-aware, expensive.

2. **Local vs global optimum guarantee**  
   - Gradient methods: Local.  
   - Heuristics: Possibly global, slow.

3. **Compliance vs stress vs frequency constraints**  
   - Compliance (stiffness): Convex, easy.  
   - Stress: Non-convex, singularity issues.  
   - Frequency: Eigenvalue problem, expensive.
