## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **Sustainable development 係 three pillars: economic, social, environmental**  
   Trade-offs 喺 pillar 之間, 而非 within a pillar。
2. **Technology transfer ≠ adoption**  
   Hardware 容易轉移, 制度 / 文化 / 維護 capacity 難。
3. **Globalization 嘅 winners 唔一定 local winners**  
   Extraction of value to multinational; local spillover uncertain。
4. **Infrastructure as a system, not a project**  
   Road without maintenance budget is a path to nowhere.
5. **Resilience > efficiency in low-resource settings**  
   Repairable, modular, locally-sourced > high-tech, efficient。

---

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **Aid effectiveness: top-down vs bottom-up**  
   - Top-down: Macro plans, donor-driven.  
   - Bottom-up: Community-led, slow, locally-fit.

2. **Free trade vs protectionism for development**  
   - Free: Efficiency, integration.  
   - Protection: Infant industry, sovereignty.

3. **Climate finance: grants vs loans**  
   - Grants: No debt burden, but limited.  
   - Loans: Scalable, debt risk.
