## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **連續介質力學是統一的框架**  
   Solids, fluids, and soft materials are all described by conservation laws + constitutive relations.

2. **變分原理與弱形式是計算力學的基礎**  
   Almost every modern numerical method starts from a weak statement of the balance laws.

3. **客觀性與材料標架無關性是本構的硬性要求**  
   Constitutive models must be independent of the observer; this constrains admissible forms.

4. **尺度分離允許均質化**  
   When scales are well separated, macroscopic behaviour can be derived from microscopic RVE analysis.

5. **穩定性與唯一性不是自動保證的**  
   Loss of ellipticity, localization, and non-uniqueness appear in softening and finite-strain problems.

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **唯象本構 vs 微觀力學推導本構**  
   - Phenomenological: Practical, calibrated to tests.  
   - Micromechanical: Provides physical insight and better extrapolation.

2. **小應變理論 vs 有限應變理論作為預設起點**  
   - Small strain: Sufficient and simpler for most civil structures.  
   - Finite strain: Necessary for soft materials, forming, and large-displacement problems.

3. **解析洞察 vs 大規模計算**  
   - Analytical: Builds intuition and provides benchmarks.  
   - Computational: Solves problems of realistic complexity.
