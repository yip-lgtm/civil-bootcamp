## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **Sustainability 係 multi-scale**  
   Material → product → building → city → planet. Each scale 嘅 dynamics 不同。
2. **LCA 嘅 system boundary 決定結論**  
   Cradle-to-grave vs cradle-to-gate: scope choice drives results.
3. **Planetary boundaries 框架**  
   9 boundaries (climate, biodiversity, nitrogen, etc.) define safe operating space.
4. **Strong vs weak sustainability**  
   - Weak: Substitutability between capitals.  
   - Strong: Critical natural capital is non-substitutable.
5. **Decoupling 嘅兩個維度**  
   Resource decoupling (less per unit GDP) vs impact decoupling (less per unit resource).

---

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **Green growth vs degrowth**  
   - Green growth: Technology can decouple GDP from impact.  
   - Degrowth: Absolute reduction needed; growth itself is the problem.

2. **Carbon offsets vs direct reduction**  
   - Offsets: Cheap, scalable, integrity questions.  
   - Direct: Costly, certain.

3. **Individual action vs systemic change**  
   - Individual: Visible, limited impact.  
   - Systemic: Highest leverage, politically harder.

## 問題 3：生成 10 個能區分深度理解與死背知識的問題

1. 解釋「embodied carbon」vs「operational carbon」嘅 trade-off 喺 building design。
2. 為什麼 LCA 對建材 comparison 容易, 但對 whole-building assessment 難？
3. 給定一個 concrete structure, 計算 cradle-to-gate GWP。
4. 解釋 planetary boundaries 嘅 9 categories 同其 current status (Rockström 2009 update)。
5. 為什麼「circular economy」對 cement industry 比 steel 更難實現？
6. 解釋「scope 1, 2, 3 emissions」對 construction company 嘅 actual carbon footprint。
7. 給定一個 city, 設計 material flow analysis (MFA) framework。
8. 為什麼 sustainability assessment (LEED, BREEAM, WELL) 唔能夠 capture all 嘅 externalities？
9. 解釋 Doughnut Economics 嘅 social floor + ecological ceiling 框架。
10. 比較 LEED v4 同 LEED v5 嘅 重大 changes (especially embodied carbon)。
