## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **地質時間尺度 vs 工程時間尺度**  
   Geology: millions of years; engineering: decades. Sizing up long-term processes is fundamental.
2. **岩石力學 vs 土力學**  
   Rocks: discontinuum, jointed, anisotropic. Soils: continuum, frictional, dilative.
3. **地質不確定性 (Geological uncertainty)**  
   Subsurface conditions are never fully known; site investigation is sampling.
4. **Discontinuities control behavior**  
   Joints, faults, bedding planes — not intact rock — typically dominate.
5. **Water is the agent of failure**  
   Most geological failures (landslides, sinkholes, piping) involve water; pore pressure is key.

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **Continuum vs discontinuum modeling**  
   - Continuum (FEM): Easier, doesn't capture joints.  
   - Discontinuum (DEM, DFN): Captures joints, expensive.

2. **Empirical vs analytical rock mass classification**  
   - Empirical (RMR, Q, GSI): Fast, field-based.  
   - Analytical: Theoretical, model-specific.

3. **Deterministic vs probabilistic site characterization**  
   - Deterministic: Single best-estimate profile.  
   - Probabilistic: Distribution of profiles, Monte Carlo.
