## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **Vector space = 線性組合嘅 structure**  
   Basis, dimension, span — the geometry of linear algebra。
2. **Matrix 係 linear transformation**  
   $A: V \to W$; rank, null space, range。
3. **Eigen-decomposition 解構**  
   $A v = \lambda v$ — direction-preserving stretches。
4. **Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) 係 universal**  
   Any matrix $A = U \Sigma V^T$; PCA, low-rank, pseudoinverse。
5. **Numerical stability matters**  
   Condition number, pivoting, iterative refinement。

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **Geometric vs algebraic approach**  
   - Geometric (Strang): Visual, conceptual.  
   - Algebraic: Abstract, generalizable.

2. **Computational vs theoretical**  
   - Computational: Algorithms (LAPACK, NumPy).  
   - Theoretical: Proofs, structure.

3. **Continuous vs discrete**  
   - Continuous: Infinite-dim, functional analysis.  
   - Discrete: Matrices, finite-dim.

8. 給定一個 graph adjacency matrix, 計算 second eigenvalue 同 graph connectivity 嘅 relationship。
