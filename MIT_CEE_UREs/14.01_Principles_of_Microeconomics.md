## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **Supply & demand 決定 market equilibrium**  
   Price discovery through voluntary exchange。
2. **Marginal analysis 決定 decisions**  
   Optimize at margin; sunk cost irrelevant。
3. **Externalities 造成 market failure**  
   Pollution, traffic — social cost ≠ private cost。
4. **Incentives drive behavior**  
   Policy design considers response。
5. **Public goods 同 common-pool resources 嘅 special challenges**  
   Non-rival + non-excludable → under-provision; common-pool → tragedy。

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **Free market vs government intervention**  
   - Free: Hayek, Chicago school.  
   - Intervention: Keynes, regulation.

2. **Coase theorem applicability**  
   - Strong: Bargaining solves externality.  
   - Limited: Transaction costs, free-rider.

3. **Behavioral vs rational actor models**  
   - Behavioral (Thaler): Cognitive biases.  
   - Rational: Homo economicus.

## 問題 3：生成 10 個能區分深度理解與死背知識的問題

1. 給定一個 carbon tax, 預測 industry response 同 environmental impact。
2. 解釋「Pigouvian tax」同「cap-and-trade」對 externality correction 嘅 trade-off。
3. 為什麼「tragedy of the commons」對 climate, fisheries, water 重要？
4. 解釋「public good」vs「club good」vs「common-pool」對 provision mechanism 嘅 implications。
5. 給定一個 monopoly market, 計算 deadweight loss 同 regulation options。
6. 為什麼「price elasticity」對 transportation pricing 重要 (e.g., congestion pricing)？
7. 解釋「Coase theorem」對 property rights 嘅 reliance。
8. 給定一個 game theory scenario (e.g., prisoner's dilemma), 設計 policy 改變 equilibrium。
9. 為什麼「discount rate」對 climate policy (intergenerational) 重要？
10. 設計一個 economic policy analysis 對一個 real externality (e.g., carbon, water, traffic)。
