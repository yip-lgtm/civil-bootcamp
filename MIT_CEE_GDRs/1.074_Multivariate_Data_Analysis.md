## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **資料是帶噪聲的模型輸出**  
   Data are samples from a process, not exact values; every statistic is a point estimate with uncertainty.
2. **變數之間的相關不等於因果**  
   Correlation is symmetric and easy to measure; causation requires design (intervention) or strong assumptions.
3. **高維資料的本質維度比 N 小**  
   Most real datasets have a few latent factors that drive most variation (PCA, factor analysis).
4. **模型選擇是偏誤–變異的權衡**  
   Simple models bias; complex models overfit; cross-validation reveals the sweet spot.
5. **先看資料再選模型**  
   Distribution, outliers, and missingness inform model choice — never blindly apply an algorithm.

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **頻率派 vs 貝氏派**  
   - Frequentist: p-values, confidence intervals, objective hypothesis testing.  
   - Bayesian: posterior distributions, prior knowledge, natural decision theory.

2. **假設檢定 vs 效應量 + 信賴區間**  
   - p-value: Simple, dichotomous.  
   - Effect size + CI: Magnitude matters, replication-friendly.

3. **線性 vs 非線性/機器學習模型**  
   - Linear: Interpretable, well-understood, robust.  
   - ML: Higher accuracy, harder to interpret, may overfit.
