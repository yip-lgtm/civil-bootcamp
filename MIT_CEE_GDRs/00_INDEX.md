# MIT_CEE_GDRs — General Department Requirements
**MIT Course 1-ENG | 54 Units**

---

The General Department Requirements (GDRs) are the 54 units of coursework that
every CEE undergraduate must complete, **in addition to** the GIRs and the
chosen Core Track. They form the technical foundation common to all CEE
specializations.

**Source:** [MIT CEE Undergraduate Program](https://cee.mit.edu/education/undergraduate/)

## Required subjects

| Course | Title | Units | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18.03 | Differential Equations | 12 | [18.03_Differential_Equations.md](./18.03_Differential_Equations.md) |
| 1.000 | Computer Programming for Engineers | 6 | [1.000_Computer_Programming.md](./1.000_Computer_Programming.md) |

## Typical GDR structure (54 units)

| Category | Units | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics beyond 18.03 | 12 | Statistics, probability, numerical methods |
| Computation | 6–12 | Programming, data science |
| Physics/Chem/Bio | 12–24 | Foundation sciences |
| CEE core electives | 12–18 | Intro courses across CEE tracks |

## Self-study path

1. **Year 1–2:** GIRs (parallel with GDRs)
2. **Year 2:** Complete 18.03 + 1.000 early — they unlock all later CEE courses
3. **Year 2–3:** Remaining GDRs while exploring tracks
4. **Year 3+:** Commit to a Core Track

> **Tip:** 18.03 and 1.000 are **prerequisites for nearly every CEE course** —
> complete them first.
