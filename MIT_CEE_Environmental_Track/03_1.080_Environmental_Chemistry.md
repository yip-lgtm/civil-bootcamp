## 1. 化學平衡與動力學共同決定歸宿
## Thermodynamic Equilibrium and Kinetics Together Determine Fate

**Thermodynamics tells you where the system wants to go; kinetics tells you how fast it gets there.**

環境化學的核心二分法：熱力學給出**方向與極限**（最終狀態），動力學給出**速率與時間尺度**。兩者缺一不可——只懂熱力學的工程師會設計「能反應但要等一萬年」的系統；只懂動力學的會把不可能的反應寫進模型。  
The core dichotomy of environmental chemistry: thermodynamics gives **direction and limits** (final state); kinetics gives **rate and timescale**. Both are essential — an engineer who only knows thermodynamics will design a "reactive but takes 10,000 years" system; one who only knows kinetics will put impossible reactions into the model.

### 1.1 熱力學的本質：自由能與平衡常數
### Thermodynamics: Free Energy and Equilibrium Constant

任何反應的可行性由 **Gibbs 自由能** 決定：  
The feasibility of any reaction is determined by the **Gibbs free energy**:

$$\Delta G = \Delta G^\circ + RT \ln Q$$

- $\Delta G < 0$：反應自發進行  
  Reaction proceeds spontaneously
- $\Delta G = 0$：平衡（$Q = K$）  
  Equilibrium ($Q = K$)
- $\Delta G > 0$：反應非自發  
  Reaction is non-spontaneous

平衡常數 $K$ 與 $\Delta G^\circ$ 通過 $K = e^{-\Delta G^\circ / RT}$ 相關。  
Equilibrium constant $K$ is related to $\Delta G^\circ$ via $K = e^{-\Delta G^\circ / RT}$.

**對環境化學的含意 / Implications for environmental chemistry:**

- 知道 $K$ 就能預測**最終濃度比**（如配體絡合、沉澱/溶解）
  Knowing $K$ lets us predict **final concentration ratios** (complexation, dissolution/precipitation)
- 但無法預測**多快達到平衡**
  But cannot predict **how fast equilibrium is reached**

### 1.2 動力學的本質：反應速率方程
### Kinetics: Rate Equations

零階反應 Zero order: $r = k$；半衰期 $t_{1/2} = C_0 / (2k)$

一階反應 First order: $r = kC$；$C(t) = C_0 e^{-kt}$；$t_{1/2} = \ln 2 / k$

二階反應 Second order: $r = kC_1 C_2$；動力學更複雜

米氏動力學 Michaelis–Menten: $r = \dfrac{V_{\max} C}{K_m + C}$（酶、微生物降解）

**速率常數 $k$ 的溫度依賴 / Temperature dependence (Arrhenius):**
$$k = A e^{-E_a / RT}$$

- 典型環境反應 $E_a$：40–100 kJ/mol
  Typical environmental $E_a$: 40–100 kJ/mol
- 溫度每升 10°C，$k$ 增 2–4 倍（"Q₁₀ rule"）
  Every 10°C rise doubles to quadruples $k$ ("Q₁₀ rule")

### 1.3 平衡 vs 動力學的工程決策
### Engineering Decision: Equilibrium vs Kinetics

| 情境 Scenario | 適用方法 Suitable Method | 原因 Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 地下水修復（時間尺度：年–世紀）<br>Groundwater remediation (years–centuries) | 平衡為主 Equilibrium-dominant | 時間充裕，達到平衡<br>Ample time to reach equilibrium |
| 河流污染事故（時間尺度：小時–天）<br>River pollution accident (hours–days) | 動力學為主 Kinetics-dominant | 流動快速，未達平衡<br>Fast flow, far from equilibrium |
| 飲用水消毒 Disinfection | 動力學 Kinetics | 接觸時間有限（分鐘）<br>Contact time limited (minutes) |
| 沉澱/溶解反應 Precipitation/dissolution | 平衡為主 Equilibrium-dominant | 礦物反應通常快速達到局部平衡<br>Mineral reactions usually reach local equilibrium |
| 微生物降解 Microbial degradation | 米氏 Michaelis–Menten | 受基質濃度與微生物活性雙重控制<br>Controlled by both substrate and biomass |
| 吸附/解吸 Adsorption/desorption | 兩者都 Both | 化學吸附（慢）vs 物理吸附（快）<br>Chemosorption (slow) vs physisorption (fast) |

### 1.4 平衡與動力學的耦合案例：碳酸鹽系統
### Coupled Equilibrium–Kinetics: The Carbonate System

$\mathrm{CO_2 (g)} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{CO_2 (aq)} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{H_2CO_3} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{H^+} + \mathrm{HCO_3^-} \rightleftharpoons 2\mathrm{H^+} + \mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}$

- 平衡常數 $K_1$、$K_2$ 給出最終形態分佈（熱力學）
  Equilibrium constants $K_1$, $K_2$ give final speciation (thermodynamics)
- 但 $\mathrm{CO_2 \leftrightarrow HCO_3^-}$ 的水合反應 $\mathrm{CO_2 + H_2O \rightarrow H_2CO_3}$ 較慢（動力學瓶頸）
  But the hydration $\mathrm{CO_2 + H_2O \rightarrow H_2CO_3}$ is slow (kinetic bottleneck)
- 工程含意：脫碳塔設計需考慮水合速率，不能假設瞬時平衡
  Engineering: decarbonation tower design must consider hydration rate, not instantaneous equilibrium

### 1.5 專家如何使用這個模型
### How Experts Use This Mental Model

- **先確認「能反應嗎」**（查 $\Delta G$ 或 $K$）→ 再問「多快」（查 $k$）  
  First confirm "can it react?" ($\Delta G$ or $K$) → then "how fast?" ($k$)
- **時間尺度匹配是設計核心**：反應時間 vs 系統停留時間  
  Time-scale matching is the core of design: reaction time vs system residence time
- **不要把「應該」反應當成「已經」反應**：很多環境系統永遠未達平衡  
  Don't equate "should react" with "has reacted": many environmental systems are never at equilibrium
- **長期評估用平衡，短期響應用動力學**  
  Long-term assessment uses equilibrium; short-term response uses kinetics

### 1.6 深度自測問題
### Deep Self-Test Questions

1. 為什麼在設計飲用水消毒系統時，幾乎從不考慮熱力學平衡，而必須用動力學？  
   Why do drinking-water disinfection designs almost never use thermodynamic equilibrium, but always kinetics?
2. 在地下水礦物溶解問題中，為什麼「平衡假設」常常合理，但「瞬時平衡」有時不合理？  
   In groundwater mineral dissolution, why is the "equilibrium assumption" often reasonable, but "instantaneous equilibrium" sometimes not?
3. Q₁₀ = 2–4 意味著什麼？對氣候變遷下的環境化學意味著什麼？  
   What does Q₁₀ = 2–4 imply? What does it mean for environmental chemistry under climate change?
4. 為什麼在深海（停留時間 > 1000 年）很多反應可視為達到平衡，但表層海（停留時間 ~ 1 年）不行？  
   Why are many reactions at equilibrium in the deep ocean (residence time > 1000 years) but not in the surface ocean (~1 year)?

### 核心結論 / Core Takeaway

> **熱力學告訴你「最終形態」，動力學告訴你「什麼時候」。沒有時間尺度的化學反應是無意義的。**  
> **Thermodynamics tells you the "final state"; kinetics tells you "when." A chemical reaction without a timescale is meaningless.**

## 2. pH、氧化還原電位、絡合作用是三大控制變數
## pH, Eh, and Complexation Are the Three Master Variables

**Most environmental reactions are governed by these master variables.**

環境化學中，幾乎所有反應的平衡與速率都由這三個變數（或其組合）控制。它們決定了元素的形態、毒性、生物可利用性。  
In environmental chemistry, nearly all reaction equilibria and rates are controlled by these three variables (or combinations thereof). They determine element speciation, toxicity, and bioavailability.

### 2.1 pH：酸度作為形態控制者
### pH: Acidity as Speciation Controller

pH = $-\log a_{H^+}$；每變化 1 單位代表 10 倍的 H⁺ 活度變化。  
pH = $-\log a_{H^+}$; each unit change means 10× change in H⁺ activity.

**pH 對形態的影響 / pH effects on speciation:**

弱酸 HA：$\alpha_0 = \dfrac{[H^+]}{[H^+] + K_a}$，$\alpha_1 = \dfrac{K_a}{[H^+] + K_a}$

- pH < pKa：主要以 HA 形式存在
  pH < pKa: mainly as HA
- pH > pKa：主要以 A⁻ 形式存在
  pH > pKa: mainly as A⁻

**例子 / Examples:**

| 弱酸 Weak acid | pKa | pH 5 形態 Form at pH 5 | pH 8 形態 Form at pH 8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 醋酸 Acetic acid | 4.76 | 35% HA, 65% A⁻ | ~100% A⁻ |
| 苯酚 Phenol | 9.95 | ~100% HA | ~10% A⁻ |
| 氨水 Ammonia | 9.25 | ~100% NH₄⁺ | ~6% NH₄⁺, 94% NH₃ |

**工程含意 / Engineering implication:** 改變 pH 可控制污染物的揮發性、吸附性、生物毒性。  
Changing pH can control pollutant volatility, adsorption, and biological toxicity.

### 2.2 氧化還原電位 (Eh)：電子活性
### Eh (Redox Potential): Electron Activity

$$Eh = E^\circ - \frac{RT}{nF} \ln Q$$

- 對單電子反應：每變化 59 mV 代表 10 倍電子活度變化
  For single-electron reaction: each 59 mV = 10× change in electron activity
- 高 Eh：氧化環境（如好氧地下水、+300 至 +600 mV）
  High Eh: oxidizing (e.g., aerobic groundwater, +300 to +600 mV)
- 低 Eh：還原環境（如厭氧含水層，-200 至 0 mV）
  Low Eh: reducing (e.g., anaerobic aquifer, -200 to 0 mV)

**典型氧化還原序列 / Typical redox sequence (高 Eh → 低 Eh):**

O₂/ H₂O → NO₃⁻/ N₂ → Mn⁴⁺/ Mn²⁺ → Fe³⁺/ Fe²⁺ → SO₄²⁻/ H₂S → CO₂/ CH₄

**工程應用 / Engineering applications:**

- **飲用水處理**：曝氣 → Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺ → 沉澱
  Drinking water: aeration → Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺ → precipitation
- **地下水修復**：注入電子受體（如硝酸鹽）刺激生物降解
  Groundwater remediation: inject electron acceptors (e.g., nitrate) to stimulate biodegradation
- **金屬腐蝕**：低 Eh 易腐蝕
  Metal corrosion: low Eh favors corrosion

### 2.3 絡合：配位化學決定生物可利用性
### Complexation: Coordination Chemistry Controls Bioavailability

金屬離子 M^n+ 與配體 L 形成絡合物 ML_x：  
Metal ion M^n+ forms complex with ligand L:

$$M^{n+} + xL \rightleftharpoons ML_x, \quad K_f = \frac{[ML_x]}{[M^{n+}][L]^x}$$

**為什麼重要 / Why it matters:**

- 自由金屬離子才是生物可利用的、毒性的形態
  Free metal ion is the bioavailable, toxic form
- 絡合後毒性降低，但可改變遷移性
  Complexed form has lower toxicity but different mobility

**典型配體 / Typical ligands:**

- 無機 Inorganic: Cl⁻, OH⁻, CO₃²⁻, S²⁻, NH₃
- 有機 Organic: 腐殖酸、富里酸 (FA, HA)、EDTA、檸檬酸
  Organic: humic/fulvic acid, EDTA, citric acid

**例子 / Example:** 銅在水中的形態
$$\mathrm{Cu^{2+} + CO_3^{2-} \rightleftharpoons CuCO_3 (aq)}$$
$$\mathrm{Cu^{2+} + 2OH^- \rightleftharpoons Cu(OH)_2 (s)}$$

隨 pH 升高，Cu 從自由離子 → 碳酸鹽絡合物 → 氫氧化物沉澱。  
As pH rises, Cu goes from free ion → carbonate complex → hydroxide precipitate.

### 2.4 三大變數的相互依賴
### Mutual Dependence of the Three Variables

| 變數 Variable | 依賴於 Depends on | 影響 Affects |
|---|---|---|
| pH | 碳酸系統、強弱酸、緩衝<br>Carbonate system, weak/strong acids, buffers | 金屬形態、吸附、毒性<br>Metal speciation, adsorption, toxicity |
| Eh | 氧供應、有機物、微生物活性<br>O₂ supply, organic matter, microbial activity | 金屬形態、營養鹽形態<br>Metal speciation, nutrient forms |
| 絡合 Complexation | pH、Eh、配體濃度、金屬總量<br>pH, Eh, ligand, total metal | 自由金屬活度、毒性、遷移<br>Free metal activity, toxicity, mobility |

**三個同時改變 → 形態完全改變**  
**All three changing simultaneously → complete speciation shift**

### 2.5 專家如何使用這個模型
### How Experts Use This Mental Model

- **測量是起點**：先量 pH、Eh，再做任何化學推論
  Measurement comes first: measure pH and Eh before any chemical inference
- **改變 pH/Eh 是一種處理手段**：用石灰調節 pH、曝氣調節 Eh
  Changing pH/Eh is a treatment: lime to adjust pH, aeration to adjust Eh
- **絡合效應會反轉毒性預期**：EDTA 污染下，重金屬「看起來不毒」但仍可生物累積
  Complexation can reverse toxicity expectations: under EDTA contamination, heavy metals "appear non-toxic" but still bioaccumulate
- **建化學形態模型前必須先確定三變數的時空分佈**
  Before speciation modeling, determine spatiotemporal distribution of the three master variables

### 2.6 深度自測問題
### Deep Self-Test Questions

1. 在好氧地下水中加入有機污染物後，pH、Eh 會如何演變？金屬形態如何跟著變？  
   In aerobic groundwater after adding organic pollutants, how do pH and Eh evolve? How does metal speciation follow?
2. 為什麼「金屬總濃度」作為環保標準是誤導性的？應改用什麼？  
   Why is "total metal concentration" misleading as an environmental standard? What should replace it?
3. 在含硫厭氧環境中，pH 5 和 pH 8 下重金屬的遷移性差異為何？  
   In sulfur-containing anaerobic environments, why does heavy-metal mobility differ between pH 5 and pH 8?
4. 為什麼水處理中常加入「絡合劑」（如檸檬酸）來螯合金屬？這會帶來什麼副作用？  
   Why are chelators (e.g., citric acid) often added in water treatment? What side effects result?

### 核心結論 / Core Takeaway

> **pH、Eh、絡合是化學形態的「三位一體」。單獨看一個，永遠只看到一半的故事。**  
> **pH, Eh, and complexation are the "trinity" of speciation. Looking at one alone always gives only half the story.**

## 3. 界面過程往往比本體反應更重要
## Interfacial Processes Often Dominate Bulk-Phase Reactions

**Adsorption, precipitation, and biofilm reactions at surfaces dominate contaminant fate.**

在環境系統中，**真正的反應發生在界面上**：固–液（吸附）、液–氣（揮發）、液–液（分配）、固–生物膜（生物降解）。忽略界面過程的模型，會嚴重低估污染物的滯留與轉化。  
In environmental systems, **the real reactions occur at interfaces**: solid–liquid (adsorption), liquid–gas (volatilization), liquid–liquid (partitioning), solid–biofilm (biodegradation). Models that ignore interfacial processes severely underestimate retention and transformation.

### 3.1 為什麼界面重要：兩大原因
### Why interfaces matter: two reasons

**(a) 表面能 / Surface energy:**

表面分子有未飽和鍵，表面張力高 → 反應活性高。  
Surface molecules have unsaturated bonds, high surface energy → high reactivity.

**(b) 微生物聚集 / Microbial colonization:**

95% 以上的環境微生物附著在固體表面形成生物膜。  
>95% of environmental microbes are attached to solid surfaces as biofilms.

### 3.2 吸附等溫線
### Adsorption Isotherms

**Langmuir 單層吸附 / Langmuir monolayer adsorption:**

$$q_e = \frac{q_m K_L C_e}{1 + K_L C_e}$$

- $q_e$：吸附量 (mg/g)；$C_e$：平衡濃度 (mg/L)
  $q_e$: adsorbed amount (mg/g); $C_e$: equilibrium concentration (mg/L)
- $q_m$：最大吸附容量 (mg/g)
- $K_L$：Langmuir 常數，與結合能相關
- **假設**：均勻表面、單層、有限位點
  **Assumptions**: uniform surface, monolayer, finite sites

**Freundlich 經驗式 / Freundlich empirical:**

$$q_e = K_F C_e^{1/n}$$

- $1/n < 1$：有利吸附；$1/n = 1$：線性
  $1/n < 1$: favorable; $1/n = 1$: linear
- **假設**：異質表面、經驗關係
  **Assumptions**: heterogeneous surface, empirical

**線性分配 / Linear partitioning (低濃度極限):**

$$q_e = K_d C_e$$

- $K_d$：分配係數 (L/kg)
- 低濃度下 Freundlich 退化為線性

**實用建議 / Practical guidance:**

| 情境 Scenario | 推薦 Recommended | 原因 Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 重金屬高濃度 Heavy metals, high conc. | Langmuir | 飽和效應明顯<br>Saturation matters |
| 有機物低濃度 Organic, low conc. | 線性 $K_d$ | 簡單足夠<br>Simple suffices |
| 異質表面 Heterogeneous surface | Freundlich | 經驗性涵蓋非線性<br>Empirical captures nonlinearity |
| 含腐殖酸 With humic acid | 包含 $K_{oc}$ | 有機碳歸一化<br>Organic carbon normalization |

### 3.3 遲滯效應 (Hysteresis)
### Hysteresis

吸附與解吸**不等**——解吸通常比吸附慢、滯留更多。  
Adsorption and desorption are **not equal** — desorption is usually slower and retains more.

**原因 / Reasons:**

- 老化 (Aging)：污染物進入土壤微孔，物理上難以解吸
  Aging: pollutants enter soil micropores, physically hard to desorb
- 化學鍵形成 (Chemical bonding)：共價鍵、不可逆吸附
  Chemical bonding: covalent, irreversible
- 生物降解過程中產生代謝物，可能更強吸附
  Metabolites may bind more strongly

**工程含意 / Engineering implication:** 用吸附實驗預測修復時間常**過於樂觀**。  
Using adsorption experiments to predict remediation time is often **too optimistic**.

### 3.4 生物膜反應：非均相反應器
### Biofilm Reactions: Heterogeneous Reactors

生物膜是微生物 + EPS（胞外聚合物） + 水的三相結構：  
Biofilm is a three-phase structure of microbes + EPS + water:

```
基質 (水)  →  [邊界層]  →  [生物膜]  →  [固體載體]
Substrate  →  Boundary  →  Biofilm  →  Solid carrier
                layer
```

**反應–擴散耦合 / Reaction–diffusion coupling:**

$$\frac{\partial C}{\partial t} = D_b \frac{\partial^2 C}{\partial z^2} - r(C)$$

- 在生物膜內，底物濃度隨深度衰減
  Substrate concentration decays with depth into the biofilm
- 有效反應速率受限於**擴散**
  Effective rate is **diffusion-limited**

**生物膜厚度臨界值 / Critical biofilm thickness:**

$$L_c = \sqrt{\frac{2 D_b C_0}{r_{\max}}}$$

超過 $L_c$，內層生物無法獲得底物 → 死亡 → 生物膜脫落。  
Beyond $L_c$, inner cells starve → die → biofilm sloughs.

**工程應用 / Engineering applications:**

- 滴濾池、旋轉生物接觸器、生物濾池、移動床生物膜反應器 (MBBR)
  Trickling filters, RBC, biofilter, MBBR
- 地下水生物修復：注入營養鹽刺激本土生物膜
  Groundwater bioremediation: inject nutrients to stimulate indigenous biofilm

### 3.5 表面絡合模型 (Surface Complexation Model, SCM)
### Surface Complexation Model (SCM)

對金屬氧化物等帶電表面，吸附通過表面絡合反應：  
For charged surfaces (e.g., metal oxides), adsorption proceeds via surface complexation:

$$\equiv \mathrm{SOH} + M^{n+} \rightleftharpoons \equiv \mathrm{SOM}^{(n-1)+} + H^+$$

- $\equiv \mathrm{SOH}$：表面羥基
- 用電雙層理論描述表面電荷
  Electric double-layer theory describes surface charge
- **MINTEQA2、PHREEQC、Visual MINTEQ** 等軟件實現
  Implemented in **MINTEQA2, PHREEQC, Visual MINTEQ**

**工程含意 / Engineering implication:**

- 預測不同 pH 下重金屬的吸附
  Predict heavy-metal adsorption at different pH
- 設計吸附劑（活性炭、沸石、改性黏土）
  Design adsorbents (activated carbon, zeolite, modified clays)

### 3.6 專家如何使用這個模型
### How Experts Use This Mental Model

- **永遠先問「固體表面」**：土壤、沉積物、生物膜——界面在那裡
  Always ask "solid surface" first: soil, sediment, biofilm — interfaces are there
- **吸附實驗要做動態批試驗**，不只是靜態平衡
  Adsorption experiments should be dynamic batch, not just static equilibrium
- **生物修復效率受限於擴散**，不是動力學
  Bioremediation efficiency is diffusion-limited, not kinetics-limited
- **修復時間預測要包含遲滯與老化**
  Remediation time predictions must include hysteresis and aging

## 4. 質量守恆與元素循環是最終約束
## Mass Conservation and Element Cycles Are the Final Constraints

**Carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus, and metal cycles must close; models that violate them are wrong.**

質量守恆是物理學最基本的約束之一。環境化學中，這意味著元素必須在**形態之間守恆**——不能憑空產生或消失。任何模型如果不滿足質量守恆，就是錯的。  
Mass conservation is one of the most fundamental constraints in physics. In environmental chemistry, this means elements must be **conserved across species** — neither created nor destroyed out of thin air. Any model that violates mass conservation is wrong.

### 4.1 元素守恆的數學形式
### Mathematical Form of Element Conservation

對元素 X，其總濃度等於所有形態的加權和：  
For element X, the total concentration equals the weighted sum of all species:

$$C_{X,total} = \sum_i c_i \cdot [S_i]$$

其中 $c_i$ 是形態 $S_i$ 中 X 的原子數。  
Where $c_i$ is the number of X atoms in species $S_i$.

**例子 / Example:** 總無機碳 (DIC, Dissolved Inorganic Carbon):
$$\mathrm{DIC} = [\mathrm{H_2CO_3}] + [\mathrm{HCO_3^-}] + [\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}]$$

總溶解磷 (TDP):
$$\mathrm{TDP} = [\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}] + [\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}] + [\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}] + [\mathrm{H_3PO_4}] + \text{有機磷} + \text{吸附態磷}$$

### 4.2 主要生物地球化學循環
### Major Biogeochemical Cycles

**碳循環 / Carbon cycle:**

大氣 CO₂ → 光合作用 → 有機碳 → 呼吸/分解 → CO₂  
Atmospheric CO₂ → photosynthesis → organic C → respiration/decomposition → CO₂

關鍵：沉積碳酸鹽（CaCO₃）作為長期儲庫  
Key: sedimentary carbonate (CaCO₃) as long-term reservoir

**氮循環 / Nitrogen cycle:**

N₂ → 固氮 (Haber, 生物) → NH₄⁺ → 硝化 (NH₄⁺ → NO₂⁻ → NO₃⁻) → 反硝化 (NO₃⁻ → N₂)  
N₂ → fixation (Haber, biological) → NH₄⁺ → nitrification → denitrification → N₂

關鍵：人為固氮（Haber-Bosch）已破壞自然平衡  
Key: anthropogenic N₂ fixation (Haber-Bosch) has disrupted natural balance

**磷循環 / Phosphorus cycle:**

岩石風化 → PO₄³⁻ → 生物吸收 → 死亡/分解 → 沉積  
Rock weathering → PO₄³⁻ → biological uptake → death/decomposition → sedimentation

關鍵：沒有大氣氣相，純地質循環  
Key: no atmospheric gas phase, purely geological

**硫循環 / Sulfur cycle:**

S²⁻ → S⁰ → SO₄²⁻（氧化）或反之（還原）  
S²⁻ → S⁰ → SO₄²⁻ (oxidation) or reverse (reduction)

關鍵：酸雨 (SO₂ 氧化 → H₂SO₄)  
Key: acid rain (SO₂ oxidation → H₂SO₄)

### 4.3 守恆約束在模型中的實現
### Conservation Constraints in Models

**地球化學模型 / Geochemical models (PHREEQC, MINTEQ):**

- 自動滿足所有形態的質量守恆
  Automatically satisfy mass conservation across all species
- 給定總濃度 + 環境條件 (pH, Eh, T, P) → 計算形態分佈
  Given total concentration + environmental conditions (pH, Eh, T, P) → compute speciation

**生物地球化學模型 / Biogeochemical models (e.g., DAYCENT, DNDC):**

- 模擬 C, N, P 池之間的流動
  Simulate flows between C, N, P pools
- 流動不能違反守恆
  Flows cannot violate conservation

**水化學耦合 (Transport + Reaction) / Coupled water chemistry:**

$$\frac{\partial C_{i,total}}{\partial t} = -v \cdot \nabla C_i + D \nabla^2 C_i + \sum_j R_{ij}$$

每個 $R_{ij}$ 必須對元素 X 加權守恆：$\sum_i c_i R_{ij} = 0$  
Each $R_{ij}$ must conserve element X: $\sum_i c_i R_{ij} = 0$

### 4.4 守恆約束的工程應用
### Engineering Applications of Conservation Constraints

**(a) 反應路徑建模 / Reaction-path modeling:**

從已知起點，追蹤滴定或反應過程中形態演變。  
From known starting point, track speciation evolution during titration or reaction.

例：加石灰到酸性礦山水 → 沉澱順序
Add lime to acid mine water → precipitation sequence

**(b) 質量平衡計算 / Mass-balance calculation:**

對污染場址：
- 總輸入 = 總輸出 + 累積 + 降解
  Total input = total output + accumulation + degradation
- 任何差異必須有解釋（漏失源/匯、不確定性）
  Any difference must be explained (missing source/sink, uncertainty)

**(c) 廢水處理廠設計 / WWTP design:**

- 質量平衡用於確定污泥產量、曝氣需求、藥劑投加量
  Mass balance used to determine sludge production, aeration demand, chemical dosing

### 4.5 專家如何使用這個模型
### How Experts Use This Mental Model

- **永遠先寫總平衡**：總進 = 總出 + 累積 + 反應
  Always start with total balance: total in = total out + accumulation + reaction
- **元素守恆是模型驗證的最後一道防線**
  Element conservation is the last line of defense in model validation
- **耦合模型中守恆約束自動檢查**：用軟件（PHREEQC）做形態計算
  In coupled models, conservation constraints are auto-checked: use software (PHREEQC) for speciation
- **不守恆的模型結果立即丟棄**
  Discard model results that violate conservation

### 核心結論 / Core Takeaway

> **守恆是物理事實，不是模型選項。任何「消失」或「憑空出現」都必須有解釋。**  
> **Conservation is a physical fact, not a model option. Any "disappearance" or "appearance out of nowhere" must be explained.**

## 5. 分析化學是所有推論的基礎
## Analytical Chemistry Is the Foundation of All Inference

**Without reliable measurement, every model and conclusion is speculative.**

模型是骨架，分析化學是血肉。再好的熱力學或動力學模型，如果沒有可靠的實測數據支持，都只是猜測。**「garbage in, garbage out」** 在環境化學中特別真實。  
Models are the skeleton; analytical chemistry is the flesh. Even the best thermodynamic or kinetic model, without reliable measurement data, is just speculation. **"Garbage in, garbage out"** is especially true in environmental chemistry.

### Speciation Analysis

測總濃度往往誤導——**形態**才是真正的毒理/環境意義。  
Measuring total concentration is often misleading — **speciation** is the true toxicological/environmental meaning.

## 詳解 1：碳酸系統作為 pH 緩衝
## Solution 1: The Carbonate System as pH Buffer

**問題 / Question:**  
為什麼碳酸系統是天然水體 pH 緩衝的核心？寫出主要反應與常數。  
Why is the carbonate system the core pH buffer in natural waters? Write the main reactions and constants.

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 主要反應 / Main reactions**

$$\mathrm{CO_2(g)} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{CO_2(aq)} \quad K_H = 3.4 \times 10^{-2} \text{ mol/(L·atm)}$$

$$\mathrm{CO_2(aq)} + \mathrm{H_2O} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{H_2CO_3} \quad [\mathrm{H_2CO_3}] = [\mathrm{CO_2(aq)}]$$

$$\mathrm{H_2CO_3} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{H^+} + \mathrm{HCO_3^-} \quad K_1 = 10^{-6.3} \text{ at 25°C}$$

$$\mathrm{HCO_3^-} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{H^+} + \mathrm{CO_3^{2-}} \quad K_2 = 10^{-10.3} \text{ at 25°C}$$

**2. 為什麼是核心緩衝 / Why it's the core buffer**

- 大氣 CO₂ 持續供應（~420 ppm → 平衡濃度 ~14 μM）
  Atmospheric CO₂ continuously supplies (constant reservoir)
- 反應快速達到平衡（除了 CO₂ 水合稍慢）
  Reaches equilibrium quickly (except CO₂ hydration, slightly slow)
- 三對共軛酸鹼對（CO₂/HCO₃⁻, HCO₃⁻/CO₃²⁻）覆蓋 pH 5–9
  Two conjugate pairs cover pH 5–9
- 海洋 DIC 庫 ~38,000 Gt C，是大氣 CO₂ 的 50+ 倍
  Ocean DIC pool ~38,000 Gt C, 50× atmospheric CO₂

**3. 緩衝容量 / Buffer capacity**

$$\beta = 2.3 C_T \frac{K_1[H^+]}{(K_1 + [H^+])^2} \cdot \frac{K_2[H^+]}{(K_2 + [H^+])^2} + [H^+] + \frac{K_w}{[H^+]}$$

最大緩衝容量出現在 pH ≈ pK₁ ≈ 6.3 附近。  
Maximum buffer capacity near pH ≈ pK₁ ≈ 6.3.

**4. 開放 vs 封閉系統 / Open vs closed system**

- **開放（與大氣接觸）：** pCO₂ 固定 → 加入酸鹵被緩衝
  Open (contact with atmosphere): pCO₂ fixed → added acid/base buffered
- **封閉（隔離大氣）：** DIC 固定 → 加入酸鹵 pH 變化大
  Closed (isolated from atmosphere): DIC fixed → added acid/base causes large pH shift

**5. 工程應用 / Engineering applications**

- **水處理：** 投加石灰調節 pH → 沉澱碳酸鹽硬度
  Water treatment: lime dosing to adjust pH → carbonate hardness precipitation
- **海洋酸化：** 大氣 CO₂ 增加 → 海洋吸收 → pH 下降
  Ocean acidification: atmospheric CO₂ rise → ocean uptake → pH decline
- **飲用水：** 腐蝕控制需 Langelier Saturation Index (LSI)
  Drinking water: corrosion control via Langelier Saturation Index (LSI)

## 詳解 2：金屬毒性與自由離子活度
## Solution 2: Metal Toxicity and Free-Ion Activity

**問題 / Question:**  
說明為何金屬離子的毒性與其自由離子活度而非總濃度更相關。  
Explain why metal toxicity is more related to its free-ion activity than to its total concentration.

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 自由離子活度模型 (FIAM) / Free-Ion Activity Model (FIAM)**

生物效應與「自由金屬離子在生物膜–水介面的活度」成正比。  
Biological effect is proportional to "free metal ion activity at the biofilm–water interface."

$$E = f(a_{M^{n+}})$$

不是 $f([M]_{total})$，而是 $f(a_{M^{n+}})$。  
Not $f([M]_{total})$, but $f(a_{M^{n+}})$.

**2. 為什麼不是總濃度 / Why not total concentration**

金屬在水中的形態：
Speciation of metal in water:

$$[M]_{total} = [M^{n+}] + [M(OH)^{(n-1)+}] + [M(CO_3)^{(n-2)+}] + [M(Cl)^{(n-1)+}] + [M(org)] + [M]_{adsorbed} + [M]_{colloid}$$

但只有自由離子能穿過生物膜通道。  
But only the free ion can cross the biological membrane channel.

**3. 經典實驗 / Classic experiments**

- **Zn 對藻類毒性**：添加 EDTA 絡合 → 毒性降低（即使總 Zn 不變）
  Zn toxicity to algae: add EDTA to complex → toxicity drops (even if total Zn unchanged)
- **Cd 對魚毒性**：高 Cl⁻（絡合 Cd²⁺）→ 毒性降低
  Cd toxicity to fish: high Cl⁻ (complexes Cd²⁺) → toxicity drops
- **酸效應**：低 pH 降低金屬吸附、增加自由離子 → 毒性增加
  Acid effect: low pH reduces metal adsorption, increases free ion → toxicity increases

**4. 形態分佈的計算 / Calculating Speciation**

用地球化學模型：
Use geochemical models:

- WHAM (Windermere Humic Aqueous Model)
- MINTEQA2 / Visual MINTEQ
- PHREEQC

**5. 對法規的含意 / Regulatory implications**

- 「總鉻」可能包括三價鉻（低毒）與六價鉻（高毒）
  "Total Cr" includes Cr(III) (low toxicity) and Cr(VI) (high toxicity)
- 環境品質標準應區分形態
  Environmental quality standards should distinguish species
- WQC (Water Quality Criteria) 越來越多用 BLM (Biotic Ligand Model)
  WQC increasingly uses Biotic Ligand Model (BLM)

**6. 例外情況 / Exceptions**

- **小分子中性絡合物**（如 HgCl₂）可被生物直接吸收
  Small neutral complexes (e.g., HgCl₂) can be directly taken up
- **納米顆粒** 通過吞噬作用進入
  Nanoparticles enter via endocytosis
- **有機金屬** (如甲基汞) 是脂溶性的，跨膜行為不同
  Organometallics (e.g., methylmercury) are lipophilic, different membrane behavior

## 詳解 3：硫酸鹽還原 vs 甲烷生成
## Solution 3: Sulfate Reduction vs Methanogenesis

**問題 / Question:**  
在厭氧環境中，硫酸鹽還原與甲烷生成如何競爭？控制因素是什麼？  
In anaerobic environments, how do sulfate reduction and methanogenesis compete? What controls them?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 兩種反應 / The two reactions**

**硫酸鹽還原 / Sulfate reduction:**
$$\mathrm{SO_4^{2-} + 2CH_2O \rightarrow H_2S + 2HCO_3^-} \quad \Delta G^\circ = -48 \text{ kJ/mol}$$

**醋酸營養型甲烷生成 / Acetoclastic methanogenesis:**
$$\mathrm{CH_3COOH \rightarrow CH_4 + CO_2} \quad \Delta G^\circ = -31 \text{ kJ/mol}$$

**氫營養型甲烷生成 / Hydrogenotrophic methanogenesis:**
$$\mathrm{4H_2 + CO_2 \rightarrow CH_4 + 2H_2O} \quad \Delta G^\circ = -131 \text{ kJ/mol}$$

**2. 為什麼競爭 / Why they compete**

兩者都消耗相同的電子供體（乙酸、H₂）。  
Both consume the same electron donors (acetate, H₂).

當 SO₄²⁻ 充足時，硫酸鹽還原菌（SRB）搶先獲取 H₂ 和醋酸，產生 H₂S。  
When SO₄²⁻ is abundant, sulfate-reducing bacteria (SRB) outcompete for H₂ and acetate, producing H₂S.

熱力學：硫酸鹽還原的 $\Delta G$ 更負 → 更有利。  
Thermodynamics: sulfate reduction has more negative $\Delta G$ → more favorable.

**3. 臨界 SO₄²⁻ 濃度 / Critical SO₄²⁻ concentration**

當 [SO₄²⁻] > ~0.1–1 mM 時，硫酸鹽還原主導。  
When [SO₄²⁻] > ~0.1–1 mM, sulfate reduction dominates.

低於此值，甲烷生成開始。  
Below this value, methanogenesis starts.

**4. 環境分帶 / Environmental zonation**

從上到下（深度增加），典型分帶：
Top to bottom (increasing depth), typical zonation:

1. O₂ 還原 (aerobic)
2. NO₃⁻ 還原 (denitrification)
3. Mn⁴⁺ 還原
4. Fe³⁺ 還原
5. SO₄²⁻ 還原 (sulfate reduction) — **H₂S 產生**
6. CH₄ 生成 (methanogenesis) — **甲烷產生**

**5. 工程含意 / Engineering implications**

**(a) 厭氧消化 / Anaerobic digestion:**

- 高 SO₄²⁻ 廢水（如海水入侵、製藥廢水）→ H₂S 抑制甲烷菌
  High-SO₄²⁻ wastewater (seawater intrusion, pharmaceutical) → H₂S inhibits methanogens
- 解決：前處理除 SO₄²⁻、控制 pH、微量金屬補充
  Solutions: pre-treat to remove SO₄²⁻, control pH, supplement trace metals

**(b) 酸性礦山水 (AMD) / Acid mine drainage:**

- 黃鐵礦氧化 + 硫酸鹽還原
  Pyrite oxidation + sulfate reduction
- 在厭氧濕地中，SRB 可處理 AMD
  In anaerobic wetlands, SRB can treat AMD

**(c) 天然氣形成 / Natural gas formation:**

- 海洋沉積物中，SO₄⁻豐富 → 上層為 SRB
  In marine sediments, SO₄²⁻ abundant → SRB above
- 只有在 SO₄²⁻ 被消耗的深層，甲烷生成才開始
  Methanogenesis only begins at depth where SO₄²⁻ is consumed
- 這就是為什麼海洋天然氣多在深層
  This is why marine natural gas is found at depth

## 詳解 4：Eh-pH 圖（Pourbaix）
## Solution 4: Eh-pH Diagrams (Pourbaix Diagrams)

**問題 / Question:**  
如何用 Eh-pH 圖（Pourbaix）預測鐵與錳的穩定形態？  
How are Eh-pH (Pourbaix) diagrams used to predict stable forms of iron and manganese?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. Pourbaix 圖的本質 / Nature of Pourbaix diagrams**

二維圖：x 軸 = pH, y 軸 = Eh  
2-D plot: x = pH, y = Eh

線代表兩形態的平衡邊界。  
Lines represent equilibrium boundaries between two species.

**2. 鐵的 Pourbaix 圖 / Iron Pourbaix diagram**

在 25°C, [Fe²⁺] = 10⁻⁵ M 條件下：
At 25°C, [Fe²⁺] = 10⁻⁵ M:

- **pH < 2, Eh > 0.7 V：** Fe³⁺（酸性氧化）  
  Acidic, oxidizing: Fe³⁺
- **pH 2–7, Eh 0–0.7 V：** Fe²⁺（酸性還原）  
  Acidic, reducing: Fe²⁺
- **pH 7–14, Eh 0–0.4 V：** Fe(OH)₂ 或 FeCO₃（中性還原）  
  Neutral, reducing: Fe(OH)₂ or FeCO₃
- **pH > 7, Eh > 0.4 V：** Fe(OH)₃ 或 Fe₂O₃·H₂O（中性氧化）  
  Neutral, oxidizing: Fe(OH)₃ or Fe₂O₃·H₂O
- **強鹼強氧化：** FeO₄²⁻  
  Strong base + strong oxidizing: FeO₄²⁻

**3. 錳的 Pourbaix 圖 / Manganese Pourbaix diagram**

類似但有差異：
Similar but with differences:

- MnO₂（軟錳礦）在更寬的 pH-Eh 範圍穩定
  MnO₂ (pyrolusite) is stable over a wider pH-Eh range
- Mn²⁺ 區域比 Fe²⁺ 大 → 錳在更廣條件下溶解
  Mn²⁺ region larger than Fe²⁺ → Mn dissolves under broader conditions
- 工程含意：地下水除錳比除鐵難
  Engineering: groundwater Mn removal is harder than Fe removal

**4. 工程應用 / Engineering applications**

**(a) 水處理：**

- 除鐵：曝氣 + 沉澱（Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺ → Fe(OH)₃↓）
  Fe removal: aeration + precipitation
- 除錳：需要更強氧化（Cl₂, KMnO₄, O₃）
  Mn removal: needs stronger oxidant

**(b) 地下水修復：**

- 注入氧氣或空氣 → 鐵錳沉澱 → 去除
  Inject O₂ or air → Fe/Mn precipitate → removed
- 在還原含水層中，自然 Eh < 0 → 鐵錳以 Fe²⁺/Mn²⁺ 存在
  In reducing aquifers, natural Eh < 0 → Fe/Mn exist as Fe²⁺/Mn²⁺

**(c) 腐蝕預測：**

- 低碳鋼在 Pourbaix 圖的 Fe²⁺ 或 Fe₃O₄ 區 → 腐蝕
  Low-carbon steel in Fe²⁺ or Fe₃O₄ region → corrosion
- 在 Fe₂O₃ 區（鈍化區）→ 腐蝕慢
  In Fe₂O₃ region (passive) → slow corrosion

**5. 限制 / Limitations**

- 假設平衡（環境常不滿足）
  Assumes equilibrium (often not satisfied)
- 不包含動力學
  Does not include kinetics
- 不包含絡合（真實水有 CO₃²⁻、有機酸等）
  Does not include complexation (real water has CO₃²⁻, organic acids)
- 恆溫恆壓假設
  Constant T, P assumption

**6. 進階 / Advanced**

可用 PHREEQC、Medusa、HSC Chemistry 等軟件繪製實際水化學的 Pourbaix 圖。  
Use software (PHREEQC, Medusa, HSC Chemistry) to plot Pourbaix diagrams for real water chemistry.

## 詳解 5：吸附等溫線假設與適用範圍
## Solution 5: Adsorption Isotherm Assumptions and Applicability

**問題 / Question:**  
說明吸附等溫線（Langmuir、Freundlich）的假設與適用範圍。  
Explain the assumptions and applicable range of adsorption isotherms (Langmuir, Freundlich).

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. Langmuir 假設 / Langmuir assumptions**

1. **均勻表面：** 所有吸附位點能量相同
   Homogeneous surface: all sites have equal energy
2. **單層吸附：** 每個位點只容納一個分子
   Monolayer: each site holds only one molecule
3. **有限位點：** 表面有 $q_m$ 總位點
   Finite sites: surface has total $q_m$ sites
4. **無吸附分子交互作用**
   No interaction between adsorbed molecules

**2. Freundlich 假設 / Freundlich assumptions**

1. **異質表面：** 能量分佈（指數衰減）
   Heterogeneous surface: energy distribution (exponential decay)
2. **多層吸附：** 但無飽和
   Multilayer: but no saturation
3. **經驗式：** 無嚴格熱力學推導
   Empirical: no rigorous thermodynamic derivation
4. **低濃度時退化為線性** $q = K_d C$
   At low concentration, reduces to linear $q = K_d C$

**3. 適用範圍對比 / Applicability comparison**

| 條件 Condition | 推薦 Recommended | 原因 Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 高濃度 + 飽和趨勢明顯<br>High concentration + clear saturation | Langmuir | $q_m$ 物理意義明確<br>$q_m$ has clear physical meaning |
| 低濃度 < 10⁻⁵ M<br>Low concentration | 線性 $K_d$ | 簡單、線性足夠<br>Simple, linear suffices |
| 異質複雜表面（土壤、沉積物）<br>Heterogeneous complex surface (soil, sediment) | Freundlich | 涵蓋非線性<br>Captures nonlinearity |
| 純化學吸附（化學鍵形成）<br>Pure chemisorption (chemical bonding) | Langmuir | 單層 + 有限位點<br>Monolayer + finite sites |
| 多分子層吸附（聚合物、染料）<br>Multilayer (polymer, dye) | BET 方程 | 多層物理<br>Multilayer physics |
| 高溫氣相吸附<br>High-T gas adsorption | Langmuir / BET | 物理機制清晰<br>Clear physics |

**4. 線性分配假設的應用 / Linear $K_d$ application**

低濃度下：$K_d$ 是常用參數  
At low concentration: $K_d$ is a common parameter

- 重金屬：$K_d$ 隨 pH、Eh 強烈變化
  Heavy metals: $K_d$ strongly varies with pH, Eh
- 有機物：常歸一化為 $K_{oc} = K_d / f_{oc}$，$f_{oc}$ 為有機碳分數
  Organic: often normalized as $K_{oc} = K_d / f_{oc}$, $f_{oc}$ = organic carbon fraction
- 線性假設的好處：簡單、便於耦合到傳輸模型
  Advantage: simple, easy to couple with transport models

**5. 滯後效應 / Hysteresis**

真實吸附常**不可逆**：
Real adsorption is often **irreversible**:

- 老化 (Aging)：進入微孔
  Aging: into micropores
- 化學鍵 Covalent bonding
- 遲滯指數 Hysteresis Index (HI) = $\dfrac{q_{desorption} - q_{adsorption}}{q_{adsorption}} \times 100\%$

**6. 實驗測定 / Experimental determination**

**批次實驗 / Batch experiment:**

1. 不同初始濃度 $C_0$
2. 固定土水比
3. 平衡後測 $C_e$
4. 計算 $q_e = (C_0 - C_e) V / m$
5. 擬合等溫線

**柱實驗 / Column experiment:**

更接近現場，但解釋較複雜。  
Closer to field, but interpretation more complex.

## 詳解 6：光化學在表層 vs 地下
## Solution 6: Photochemistry in Surface vs Groundwater

**問題 / Question:**  
為什麼光化學降解在表層水中重要，而在地下水中可忽略？  
Why is photochemical degradation important in surface water but negligible in groundwater?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 光化學基本要求 / Photochemistry prerequisites**

1. **光子到達** (Photon must reach)
2. **分子吸收** (Molecule must absorb)
3. **激發態反應** (Excited state must react)

地下水三個條件都失敗。  
In groundwater, all three fail.

**2. 光子穿透深度 / Light penetration depth**

水中光的衰減：
Light attenuation in water:

$$I(z) = I_0 e^{-K_d z}$$

- $K_d$：漫射衰減係數 (m⁻¹)
  $K_d$: diffuse attenuation coefficient (m⁻¹)
- 純水：$K_d$ ≈ 0.05 m⁻¹（深紫外可達 100 m）
  Pure water: $K_d$ ≈ 0.05 m⁻¹ (deep UV can reach 100 m)
- 富營養化水：$K_d$ ≈ 2–10 m⁻¹（UV 在 1 m 內衰減 e⁻²–¹⁰）
  Eutrophic water: $K_d$ ≈ 2–10 m⁻¹ (UV decays by e⁻²–¹⁰ within 1 m)
- 濁水、含溶解有機物 (CDOM) → 衰減更快
  Turbid water, with CDOM → faster decay

**3. 表層水光化學 / Surface water photochemistry**

- 陽光提供 UV-A (315–400 nm)、UV-B (280–315 nm)
  Sunlight provides UV-A, UV-B
- CDOM 吸收 UV → 產生 ROS（活性氧物種：¹O₂, ·OH, O₂⁻·）
  CDOM absorbs UV → produces ROS (¹O₂, ·OH, O₂⁻·)
- 硝酸鹽、亞硝酸鹽光解產生 ·OH
  Nitrate, nitrite photolysis produces ·OH
- 直接光解：污染物自身吸收 UV → 分解
  Direct photolysis: pollutant absorbs UV → decomposes

**例子 / Examples:**

- 苯酚 UV → 對苯二酚、兒茶酚
  Phenol UV → hydroquinone, catechol
- 氯仿 UV → 光氣
  Chloroform UV → phosgene
- 藥物殘留 UV → 多種產物
  Pharmaceutical residues UV → multiple products

**4. 地下水中為何可忽略 / Why negligible in groundwater**

- **無 UV 光子到達**：土壤遮擋
  No UV photons reach: soil blocks
- 無 CDOM → 間接光解也少
  No CDOM → indirect photolysis also limited
- 溫度低 → 化學反應慢
  Low temperature → slow chemistry
- 微生物降解是主要歸宿
  Microbial degradation is the main fate

**例外 / Exceptions:**

- 淺層地下水（< 1 m 深度）可接收少量 UV
  Shallow groundwater (< 1 m) can receive some UV
- 喀斯特地區：地下水可暴露於陽光
  Karst regions: groundwater can be exposed to sunlight
- 砂質含水層：光可達 10–20 cm
  Sandy aquifers: light can reach 10–20 cm

**5. 工程含意 / Engineering implications**

- **飲用水處理：** 高級氧化 (AOP) 用 UV + H₂O₂ 或 UV + O₃ 模擬太陽光
  Drinking water: Advanced Oxidation Process (AOP) using UV + H₂O₂ or UV + O₃ to mimic sunlight
- **廢水深度處理：** UV / UV + H₂O₂ 降解微量污染物
  Wastewater polishing: UV / UV + H₂O₂ for micropollutant degradation
- **自然水體評估：** 必須考慮光降解作為歸宿路徑之一
  Natural water assessment: must include photolysis as a fate pathway

**6. 量子產率 (Quantum yield) / Quantum yield**

$$\Phi = \frac{\text{反應分子數}}{\text{吸收光子數}}$$

- $\Phi$ 是評估光降解效率的關鍵
  $\Phi$ is key to assessing photolysis efficiency
- 不同化合物 $\Phi$ 差異大（10⁻⁴–1）
  Wide variation across compounds (10⁻⁴–1)
- 用於計算半衰期：$t_{1/2} = 1 / (k_a \Phi)$，$k_a$ 為吸收率
  Used to compute half-life: $t_{1/2} = 1 / (k_a \Phi)$, $k_a$ = absorption rate

## 詳解 7：自然衰減可行性判斷
## Solution 7: Judging Natural Attenuation Feasibility

**問題 / Question:**  
給定一個污染場址，如何判斷自然衰減是否可行？  
For a given contaminated site, how do you judge whether natural attenuation is feasible?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 自然衰減 (Natural Attenuation, NA) 的定義 / Definition**

污染物在自然條件下，通過物理、化學、生物過程被**減少到可接受水平**。  
NA: pollutants are reduced to acceptable levels through physical, chemical, biological processes under natural conditions.

包括：
- 稀釋 (Dilution)
- 吸附 (Sorption)
- 揮發 (Volatilization)
- 生物降解 (Biodegradation)
- 化學反應 (Chemical reactions)
- 自然衰變 (Radioactive decay)

**2. 三步評估 / Three-step assessment**

**Step 1: 初步篩選 / Initial screening**

- 污染物是否在 NA 列表中？(EPA、ICE 等有清單)
  Is pollutant on the NA list? (EPA, ICE maintain lists)
- 是否有受體？(飲用水井、生態敏感區)
  Are there receptors? (drinking-water wells, ecologically sensitive areas)
- 時間尺度是否可接受？
  Is the time scale acceptable?
- 預算是否允許主動修復？
  Does the budget allow active remediation?

**Step 2: 證據鏈建立 / Evidence line construction**

需三線證據 (ASTM 標準)：
Three lines of evidence (ASTM standard):

1. **歷史證據 (Historical):** 污染羽流隨時間縮減
   Historical: plume shrinks over time
2. **水文地化證據 (Hydrogeochemical):** 沿羽流化學梯度
   Hydrogeochemical: chemical gradients along plume
3. **現場微生物證據 (Field microbiological):** 微生物活性證據
   Field microbiological: evidence of microbial activity

**Step 3: 監測自然衰減 (MNA) / Monitored Natural Attenuation**

若三線證據都支持，實施長期監測：
If all three lines support, implement long-term monitoring:

- 定期井點採樣
- 監測羽流邊界
- 監測受體
- 設置觸發條件（若超標則介入）

**3. 關鍵指標 / Key indicators**

| 指標 Indicator | 意義 Meaning | 測量方法 Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| 污染物濃度時序<br>Contaminant time series | 是否下降 Is it decreasing? | 長期監測 |
| 電子受體消耗<br>Electron acceptor depletion | O₂ ↓, NO₃⁻ ↓, SO₄²⁻ ↓, Fe²⁺ ↑ | 化學分析 |
| 代謝產物<br>Metabolites | 中間產物是否出現 | GC-MS, LC-MS |
| 微生物族群<br>Microbial community | 降解菌是否存在 | qPCR, 16S rRNA |
| 還原條件<br>Reducing conditions | Eh 是否足夠低 | 現場 Eh 電極 |
| 總質量平衡<br>Total mass balance | 是否有源 | 源–匯計算 |

**4. 不適合 NA 的情況 / Cases where NA is unsuitable**

- 污染物是**非降解性**的（PFAS, 重金屬）
  Pollutant is **non-degradable** (PFAS, heavy metals)
- 風險**時間敏感**（飲用水井 100 m 內）
  Risk is **time-critical** (within 100 m of drinking water well)
- **污染源未控制**（仍在釋放）
  **Source not controlled** (still releasing)
- 場址**不均勻**，難以預測
  Site is **heterogeneous**, hard to predict
- 修復**時間太長**（> 30 年）
  Remediation time is **too long** (> 30 years)

**5. 案例 / Case example: TCE 污染含水層**

- TCE 可在厭氧條件下降解為 cis-DCE, VC, ethene
  TCE can degrade to cis-DCE, VC, ethene under anaerobic conditions
- 監測：TCE ↓, cis-DCE ↑（早期）, 最終 ethene ↑
  Monitor: TCE ↓, cis-DCE ↑ (early), eventually ethene ↑
- 風險：VC（氯乙烯）比 TCE **更毒**——必須監測
  Risk: VC is more toxic than TCE — must monitor

**6. 報告要素 / Required report elements**

- 概念模型 (Conceptual Site Model, CSM)
- 三線證據
- 不確定性評估
- 監測計劃
- 觸發條件與應變計劃
- 預期修復時間

## 詳解 8：升溫對有機污染物的影響
## Solution 8: Temperature Effects on Organic Pollutants

**問題 / Question:**  
在氣候變遷下，升溫如何影響有機污染物的亨利常數與揮發？  
Under climate change, how does warming affect organic pollutants' Henry's law constants and volatilization?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 亨利常數的溫度依賴 / Temperature dependence of Henry's constant**

$$H(T) = H_{ref} \exp\left[-\frac{\Delta H_{vap}}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T} - \frac{1}{T_{ref}}\right)\right]$$

- $\Delta H_{vap}$：汽化焓 (J/mol)，正數
  $\Delta H_{vap}$: enthalpy of vaporization (J/mol), positive
- $H$ 隨溫度上升而**指數增加**
  $H$ increases **exponentially** with temperature
- 範例：苯 $H$ 從 10°C 到 30°C 增加約 2–3 倍
  Example: benzene $H$ from 10°C to 30°C increases by 2–3×

**2. 對揮發的影響 / Effect on volatilization**

揮發速率：
Volatilization rate:

$$F_v = K_L (C_w - C_{w,eq})$$

其中 $C_{w,eq} = C_a / H$  
where $C_{w,eq} = C_a / H$

- 升溫 → $H$ ↑ → $C_{w,eq}$ ↓ → 濃度梯度 ↑ → 揮發加速
  Warming → $H$ ↑ → $C_{w,eq}$ ↓ → concentration gradient ↑ → volatilization accelerates

**3. 對其他過程的溫度敏感性 / Temperature sensitivity of other processes**

| 過程 Process | 溫度效應 Temperature effect | 量級 Magnitude |
|---|---|---|
| 亨利常數 $H$ | 上升 Increase | 雙倍 / 10°C |
| 蒸汽壓 Vapor pressure | 上升 Increase | 雙倍 / 5–10°C |
| 水溶解度 Solubility | 下降 Decrease | 略降 / 10°C |
| 吸附 $K_d$ | 下降 Decrease（吸熱吸附）<br>Decrease (endothermic adsorption) | 10–30% / 10°C |
| 微生物降解 $k$ | 上升 Increase (Q₁₀ ≈ 2–4) | 雙倍 / 10°C |
| 水解 Hydrolysis | 上升 Increase | 雙倍 / 10°C |
| 光解 Photolysis | 不一定 Depends | 變化複雜 |

**4. 對污染歸宿的綜合影響 / Combined effect on fate**

氣候變遷下：

- **揮發加速** → 大氣停留增加
  Volatilization accelerates → atmospheric residence increases
- **吸附減弱** → 移動性增加
  Adsorption weakens → mobility increases
- **生物降解加速** → 自然衰減增強
  Biodegradation accelerates → natural attenuation enhanced
- **大氣傳輸距離可能改變**（受風場影響）
  Atmospheric transport distance may change (wind field effects)

**5. POPs 的特殊關注 / Special concern for POPs**

持久性有機污染物 (POPs, Persistent Organic Pollutants)：
POPs (e.g., dioxins, PCBs, PBDE):

- 原本因低溫沉積在寒冷地區
  Originally deposited in cold regions due to low temperature
- 氣候變遷下，**冰凍的 POPs 重新揮發** → 「grasshopper effect」
  Under climate change, **frozen POPs re-volatilize** → "grasshopper effect"
- 北極變暖 → 二次污染源
  Arctic warming → secondary pollution source

**6. 對飲用水的影響 / Drinking water implications**

- 水庫溫度上升 → 揮發污染物增加（對湖泊水庫是好消息）
  Reservoir warming → more volatilization (good for lake/reservoir)
- 地下水管網溫度上升 → 微生物活性增加 → 腐蝕、生物膜
  Water distribution network warming → microbial activity increases → corrosion, biofilm
- 高溫下消毒副產物生成加速
  Disinfection byproducts form faster at high temperature

**7. 對管理策略的影響 / Management implications**

- 排放標準需考慮**氣候情景**而非僅歷史
  Emission standards must consider **climate scenarios** not just history
- 修復設計需考慮**未來溫度**
  Remediation design must consider **future temperature**
- 監測計劃需重新評估
  Monitoring plans need reassessment

## 詳解 9：化學形態模型與傳輸模型耦合
## Solution 9: Coupling Speciation Models with Transport Models

**問題 / Question:**  
說明如何將化學形態模型與傳輸模型耦合。  
Explain how speciation models are coupled with transport models.

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 為什麼需要耦合 / Why coupling is needed**

純傳輸模型假設化學是線性、單一形態，無法處理：
Pure transport models assume linear, single-species chemistry; cannot handle:

- 多形態污染物 (Multiple species)
- 化學反應引起的延遲 (Retardation from chemical reactions)
- 依賴形態的反應速率 (Speciation-dependent rates)
- pH/Eh 沿流動路徑的變化 (pH/Eh variation along flow path)

**2. 耦合的兩種架構 / Two coupling architectures**

**(a) 鬆耦合 (Loose coupling) / Operator splitting:**

```
迴圈 Loop:
  1. 傳輸步 Transport step: 用當前形態解傳輸方程
  2. 反應步 Reaction step: 在每個節點呼叫形態模型（如 PHREEQC）
  3. 更新形態與濃度
  4. 檢查收斂 → 不收斂重複
```

- 優點：簡單；可使用獨立軟件
  Advantage: simple; can use independent software
- 缺點：時間步長需小
  Disadvantage: requires small time steps

**(b) 全耦合 (Full coupling) / Direct substitution:**

- 將形態方程作為代數約束直接代入傳輸方程
  Substitute speciation equations as algebraic constraints into transport equations
- 優點：精確；可大時間步
  Advantage: accurate; can use large time steps
- 缺點：雅可比矩陣複雜
  Disadvantage: Jacobian matrix complex

**3. 常用軟件 / Common software**

| 軟件 Software | 能力 Capability |
|---|---|
| PHREEQC | 形態計算主力 Main speciation tool |
| MINTEQA2 / Visual MINTEQ | 形態計算 Speciation |
| PHT3D | PHREEQC + MODFLOW 耦合；含傳輸<br>PHREEQC + MODFLOW coupled |
| MIN3P | 反應性傳輸 Reactive transport |
| TOUGHREACT | 多相 + 化學 Multiphase + chemistry |
| COMSOL | 通用 PDE 平台，可手動耦合<br>General PDE platform, can manually couple |
| CrunchFlow | 反應性傳輸 Reactive transport |

**4. 數值方法 / Numerical methods**

- **算子分裂 (Operator splitting):** 分步解傳輸與反應
  Operator splitting: separate transport and reaction solves
- **GMRES / BiCGStab:** 解耦合線性系統
  GMRES / BiCGStab: solve coupled linear system
- **Newton-Raphson:** 解形態代數方程
  Newton-Raphson: solve speciation algebraic equations
- **自動微分 (Automatic differentiation):** 精確雅可比
  Automatic differentiation: exact Jacobian

**5. 案例：含水層中重金屬的傳輸 / Case: heavy-metal transport in aquifer**

問題 Problem:
- 污染源釋放 Zn²⁺
- 含水層有方解石 (CaCO₃) 可緩衝 pH
- 隨水流 Zn 移動，部分被吸附，部分沉澱

Implementation:
- 網格離散含水層（MODFLOW）
- 對流–擴散方程（MT3DMS）對每種形態 Zn 求解
- 在每個時間步，每個網格節點呼叫 PHREEQC
- 更新形態分佈

**6. 挑戰 / Challenges**

- **計算成本高**：每個時間步每個節點都要解形態問題
  High computational cost: speciation solved at every node every step
- **收斂性**：高度非線性 → 牛頓法可能不收斂
  Convergence: highly nonlinear → Newton may not converge
- **不確定性**：輸入參數（$K_d$, $K$）影響大
  Uncertainty: input parameters ($K_d$, $K$) have large effect

**7. 簡化策略 / Simplification strategies**

- 假設局部平衡 → 省去動力學
  Assume local equilibrium → skip kinetics
- 將多形態組合成「lumped species」
  Combine species into "lumped species"
- 預先計算形態對 pH 的查找表
  Pre-compute speciation-vs-pH lookup table
- 限制空間中只考慮幾個關鍵形態
  Limit to a few key species in space

## 詳解 10：微量污染物的處理挑戰
## Solution 10: Challenges in Micropollutant Treatment

**問題 / Question:**  
為什麼微量污染物（藥物、PFAS）的處理比傳統污染物更困難？  
Why is treatment of micropollutants (pharmaceuticals, PFAS) more difficult than traditional pollutants?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 為什麼難 / Why difficult**

**(a) 濃度極低 / Very low concentration:**

- 藥物在水 ng/L–μg/L
  Pharmaceuticals at ng/L–μg/L
- PFAS 在水中 ng/L–μg/L
  PFAS at ng/L–μg/L
- 比傳統污染物低 6+ 個數量級
  6+ orders of magnitude lower than traditional pollutants

**(b) 設計的「非目標」/ Designed to be "non-target":**

- 藥物：對人體有效 → 對微生物難降解
  Pharmaceuticals: effective in humans → hard for microbes to degrade
- PFAS：C-F 鍵是**最強的單鍵**之一（~485 kJ/mol）
  PFAS: C-F bond is one of the **strongest single bonds** (~485 kJ/mol)
- 設計的持久性 = 環境的持久性
  Designed persistence = environmental persistence

**(c) 多樣性 / Diversity:**

- 數千種藥物、數百種 PFAS 同時存在
  Thousands of pharmaceuticals, hundreds of PFAS simultaneously
- 物理化學性質差異大（極性、揮發性、疏水性）
  Wide physico-chemical variation (polarity, volatility, hydrophobicity)
- 單一技術難以覆蓋所有
  Single technology cannot cover all

**(d) 轉化產物 (TPs) / Transformation products:**

- 母體降解可能產生更毒的 TPs
  Parent compound degradation may produce more toxic TPs
- 例：tramadol → N-oxide（更難降解）
  Example: tramadol → N-oxide (harder to degrade)
- 監測必須包括 TPs
  Monitoring must include TPs

**(e) 監測成本高 / High monitoring cost:**

- LC-MS/MS 分析昂貴（單樣本數百–數千美元）
  LC-MS/MS analysis is expensive (hundreds to thousands USD per sample)
- 數千種化合物需要不同的前處理
  Thousands of compounds require different pre-treatments

**2. 為什麼傳統方法不足 / Why traditional methods are insufficient**

| 方法 Method | 對微量污染物效果 Effect on micropollutants |
|---|---|
| 活性污泥 Activated sludge | 對許多藥物去除率 < 50%<br>Removal < 50% for many pharmaceuticals |
| 加氯消毒 Chlorination | 對部分藥物無效；產生氯化 TPs<br>Ineffective for some; produces chlorinated TPs |
| 砂濾 Sand filtration | 幾乎無效（不吸附、不降解）<br>Almost ineffective (no adsorption, no degradation) |
| 混凝 Coagulation | 對溶解態藥物無效<br>Ineffective for dissolved pharmaceuticals |
| 臭氧 Ozonation | 部分有效；但產生溴酸鹽、TPs<br>Partially effective; produces bromate, TPs |

**3. 有效處理技術 / Effective treatment technologies**

**(a) 高級氧化 (AOP) / Advanced Oxidation:**

- O₃, O₃/H₂O₂, UV/H₂O₂, UV/TiO₂
- ·OH 攻擊大多數有機物
  ·OH attacks most organics
- 問題：高能耗；產生 TPs
  Issue: high energy; produces TPs

**(b) 活性炭吸附 / Activated carbon adsorption:**

- GAC (顆粒) 或 PAC (粉末)
  GAC (granular) or PAC (powder)
- 對疏水性藥物和大多數 PFAS 有效
  Effective for hydrophobic pharmaceuticals and most PFAS
- 對短鏈 PFAS 效果差
  Poor for short-chain PFAS

**(c) 膜技術 / Membrane technology:**

- NF / RO 對幾乎所有 PFAS 有效 (>90%)
  NF/RO effective for almost all PFAS (>90%)
- 高能耗、膜污染、濃鹽水處理
  High energy, membrane fouling, brine disposal

**(d) 離子交換 / Ion exchange:**

- 對 PFAS（陰離子）特別有效
  Particularly effective for PFAS (anions)
- AIX（陰離子交換樹脂）
  Anion exchange resin (AIX)
- 可達 sub-ng/L
  Can reach sub-ng/L

**(e) 特定 PFAS 破壞 / Specific PFAS destruction:**

- 電化學氧化 (Electrochemical oxidation)
- 等離子體 (Plasma)
- 超臨界水氧化 (SCWO)
- 機械化學球磨 (Mechanochemical ball milling)
- 仍在研究階段，商業化有限
  Still in research; limited commercialization

**4. 法規驅動 / Regulatory drivers**

- 2023 年 EPA 對 PFOA/PFOS 設定 MCL = 4 ng/L（美國首次 PFAS 國家標準）
  2023 EPA MCL for PFOA/PFOS = 4 ng/L (first US national PFAS standard)
- 歐盟「Watch List」監控多種藥物和 PFAS
  EU "Watch List" monitors multiple pharmaceuticals and PFAS
- 飲用水法規越來越嚴
  Drinking-water regulations increasingly strict

**5. 工程策略 / Engineering strategy**

**(a) 多重屏障 / Multi-barrier approach:**

源頭控制 → 高級處理 → 飲用水多級防護  
Source control → advanced treatment → multi-barrier in drinking water

**(b) 風險為基礎 / Risk-based:**

不是所有藥物都需去除，優先處理高風險：
Not all pharmaceuticals need removal; prioritize high-risk:

- 高消費量 + 高持久性 + 高生物活性
  High consumption + high persistence + high bioactivity
- 例：carbamazepine（卡馬西平）作為人類活動指標
  Example: carbamazepine as human-activity indicator

**(c) 非靶向篩選 / Non-target screening:**

- 高分辨質譜（HRMS）篩選未知污染物
  High-resolution mass spectrometry (HRMS) to screen unknowns
- 對新興污染物的預警
  Early warning for emerging contaminants

**6. 對 ICE Professional Review 的含意 / Implications for ICE Professional Review**

- 微量污染物處理是「Attribute 1: Knowledge」的核心證據點
  Micropollutant treatment is a core evidence point for "Attribute 1: Knowledge"
- 必須展示對前沿技術的理解 + 實務經驗
  Must show understanding of frontier technology + practical experience
- 需有終身學習證據（快速演進的領域）
  Lifelong learning evidence required (rapidly evolving field)

## 總結 / Summary

這十題共同指向五個深層洞見：  
These ten solutions point to five deep insights:

> **1. 熱力學告訴你「最終形態」，動力學告訴你「什麼時候」。沒有時間尺度的化學反應是無意義的。**  
> **1. Thermodynamics tells you the "final state"; kinetics tells you "when." A chemical reaction without a timescale is meaningless.**
>
> **2. pH、Eh、絡合是化學形態的「三位一體」。單獨看一個，永遠只看到一半的故事。**  
> **2. pH, Eh, and complexation are the "trinity" of speciation. Looking at one alone always gives only half the story.**
>
> **3. 真實反應發生在表面，不在溶液中。忽視界面，永遠只看到一半的故事。**  
> **3. Real reactions occur at surfaces, not in solution. Ignore interfaces, and you only see half the story.**
>
> **4. 守恆是物理事實，不是模型選項。任何「消失」或「憑空出現」都必須有解釋。**  
> **4. Conservation is a physical fact, not a model option. Any "disappearance" or "appearance out of nowhere" must be explained.**
>
> **5. 沒有可靠的測量，再精緻的模型也是猜想。先驗證數據，再做任何推論。**  
> **5. Without reliable measurement, even the most refined model is speculation. Verify data first, then infer.**
