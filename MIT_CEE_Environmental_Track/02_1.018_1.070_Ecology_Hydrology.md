## 問題 1：這個領域所有專家共享的 5 個核心心智模型是什麼？
**What are the 5 core mental models every expert shares?**

1. **生態系是能量與物質的流動網絡**  
   Primary production, consumption, decomposition, and nutrient cycling form closed or open loops that determine system productivity and resilience.

2. **水文循環是氣候系統與陸地表面的耦合器**  
   Precipitation, infiltration, runoff, evapotranspiration, and groundwater are not independent processes; they are linked through energy and water balance.

3. **非線性與閾值行為是常態**  
   Regime shifts, tipping points, and hysteresis appear in both ecological and hydrological systems under stress.

4. **尺度與異質性決定觀測與模型策略**  
   Point measurements rarely represent catchment or landscape averages; upscaling and downscaling are central problems.

5. **人類活動已成為主導的生物地球化學力量**  
   Anthropogenic nutrient loading, land-use change, and climate forcing now often exceed natural variability.

## 問題 2：這個領域的專家在哪 3 個地方存在根本分歧？各方最強的論點是什麼？

1. **還原論過程模型 vs 整體論 / 複雜系統方法**  
   - Process-based: Mechanistic, predictive under novel conditions.  
   - Holistic / complex-systems: Captures emergent properties that reductionist models miss.

2. **自然為主的恢復 vs 工程輔助的生態工程**  
   - Natural: Lower cost, higher long-term resilience.  
   - Engineered: Faster results, necessary when degradation is severe or time is limited.

3. **確定性水文模型 vs 隨機 / 資料同化方法**  
   - Deterministic: Clear process representation.  
   - Stochastic / data-assimilative: Better handles uncertainty and real-time forecasting.
