## 1. 現場是最終的實驗室
## The Field Is the Ultimate Laboratory

**Controlled lab experiments are necessary but insufficient; real systems reveal couplings and constraints that labs cannot.**

實驗室提供可重複性與因果隔離；現場提供**真實性**與**耦合**。在真實環境中，溫度、降雨、生物活動、人類干擾**同時**發生，這是實驗室無法重現的。優秀環境工程師必須能在兩種環境中工作，並理解何時用何種方法。  
The lab provides reproducibility and causal isolation; the field provides **reality** and **coupling**. In real environments, temperature, rainfall, biology, and human disturbance occur **simultaneously** — which labs cannot reproduce. Good environmental engineers must work in both, and understand when to use which.

### 1.1 實驗室 vs 現場：本質差異
### Lab vs Field: Essential Differences

| 維度 Dimension | 實驗室 Lab | 現場 Field |
|---|---|---|
| 控制 Control | 變量逐一控制 Isolated | 變量耦合 Coupled |
| 重複性 Repeatability | 高 High | 低 Low |
| 外部效度 External validity | 有限 Limited | 高 High |
| 規模 Scale | 小 Small | 真實 Real |
| 時間 Time | 短 Short | 季節–年代 Seasonal–decadal |
| 倫理 Ethics | 低風險 Low risk | 中–高風險 Medium–high risk |
| 成本 Cost | 可預算 Predictable | 不可預算 Unpredictable |
| 失敗模式 Failure mode | 重做即可 Rerun | 季節錯過、需等明年 Missed season, wait a year |
| 學習 Learning | 因果清晰 Clear causality | 模式 + 假設 Patterns + hypotheses |

### 1.2 實驗室 vs 現場的典型差異案例
### Classic Lab-vs-Field Discrepancies

**(a) 生物降解速率 / Biodegradation rates:**

- 實驗室 OECD 301B：礦化率 80% / 28 天
  Lab OECD 301B: 80% mineralization / 28 days
- 現場：可能 < 10% / 年（受溫度、競爭、基質限制）
  Field: may be < 10% / year (limited by T, competition, substrate)
- **教訓：實驗室「易降解」≠ 現場「易降解」**
  **Lesson: "Readily biodegradable" in lab ≠ "readily biodegradable" in field**

**(b) 吸附係數 $K_d$ / Sorption coefficient $K_d$:**

- 實驗室批次：乾淨礦物 → $K_d$ 明確
  Lab batch: clean mineral → $K_d$ well-defined
- 現場土壤：老化、有機質、複雜礦物 → $K_d$ 大 10–1000 倍
  Field soil: aged, organic matter, complex minerals → $K_d$ 10–1000× larger
- **教訓：「老化效應」是實驗室無法捕捉的時間效應**
  **Lesson: "Aging effect" is a time effect labs cannot capture**

**(c) 微生物群落 / Microbial community:**

- 實驗室純培養：少數菌種
  Lab pure culture: few species
- 現場：數千種，每種 < 0.1% 相對豐度
  Field: thousands of species, each < 0.1% relative abundance
- 功能冗餘 → 整體功能在擾動下穩定
  Functional redundancy → overall function is stable under perturbation

### 1.3 為什麼現場不可替代 / Why field is irreplaceable

- **稀有事件只能現場測到**：極端洪水、火災、爆發
  Rare events only observable in field: extremes, fires, outbreaks
- **長期趨勢只能現場監測**：氣候變遷、土地利用演變
  Long-term trends only monitorable in field: climate change, land use evolution
- **系統耦合只能現場觀察**：養分循環 + 水文 + 生態 + 社會的綜合
  System couplings only observable in field: nutrient + hydrology + ecology + society
- **意外發現只能現場產生**：新物種、新污染、新現象
  Unexpected discoveries only in field: new species, pollution, phenomena

### 1.4 實驗室 + 現場的協同 / Lab-Field Synergy

- **實驗室 → 機制 / Lab → mechanism:** 確定因果關係
  Lab identifies causal relations
- **現場 → 邊界條件 / Field → boundary conditions:** 量化實際範圍
  Field quantifies actual ranges
- **互校 / Cross-validation:** 兩者結果必須一致
  Cross-validation: both results must agree
- **不一致 = 學習機會** = 發現新東西
  Disagreement = learning opportunity = new discovery

### 1.5 專家如何使用這個模型
### How Experts Use This Mental Model

- **永遠先問「這在現場成立嗎？」**：在發表前，必須現場驗證
  Always ask "does this hold in the field?": must verify in field before publication
- **接受「現場永遠比實驗室複雜」**：不要強行套用實驗室結論
  Accept "the field is always more complex than the lab": don't force-fit lab conclusions
- **設計實驗時考慮現場可行性**：儀器電源、維護、抗污染
  Design experiments with field feasibility: power, maintenance, contamination resistance
- **用現場數據重新校準實驗室模型**
  Use field data to recalibrate lab-derived models

### 1.6 深度自測問題
### Deep Self-Test Questions

1. 為什麼實驗室測得的吸附係數 $K_d$ 幾乎總是小於現場觀測值？  
   Why is the lab-measured $K_d$ almost always smaller than the field-observed value?
2. 純培養的「最佳降解菌」為什麼在現場往往表現不佳？  
   Why do "best degraders" from pure culture often perform poorly in the field?
3. 哪些類型的環境問題**只能**現場研究？舉三例。  
   Which types of environmental problems can **only** be studied in the field? Give three examples.
4. 如何設計實驗使實驗室結果更容易外推到現場？  
   How can experiments be designed to make lab results more transferable to the field?

### 核心結論 / Core Takeaway

> **實驗室回答「為什麼」，現場回答「怎樣」。兩者結合才完整。**  
> **The lab answers "why"; the field answers "how." Both together is complete.**

## 2. 假設必須可被現場數據證偽
## Hypotheses Must Be Falsifiable by Field Data

**Fieldwork is not tourism; every measurement must test a specific scientific or engineering hypothesis.**

田野工作最常見的失敗模式：**收集了一堆數據但不知道為什麼**。這是「探測式」（exploratory）研究本身的風險。嚴格的科學方法要求每個觀測都對應一個可證偽的假設。  
The most common failure mode of fieldwork: **collecting lots of data without knowing why**. This is an inherent risk of exploratory research. Rigorous science demands that every observation correspond to a falsifiable hypothesis.

### 2.1 假設驅動 vs 探索性研究
### Hypothesis-Driven vs Exploratory Research

| 維度 Dimension | 假設驅動 Hypothesis-driven | 探索性 Exploratory |
|---|---|---|
| 目的 Purpose | 檢驗特定假設 Test specific hypothesis | 發現新現象 Discover new phenomena |
| 設計 Design | 預先確定 Pre-determined | 自適應 Adaptive |
| 統計 Statistics | 先計算 power，再設計 | 事後檢驗 Post-hoc |
| 發表難度 Publication | 較易（結果可解釋）<br>Easier (interpretable) | 較難（需說服）<br>Harder (need to convince) |
| 風險 Risk | 假設錯了 → 浪費整個田野<br>Wrong hypothesis → entire field trip wasted | 可能「無聊」<br>May be "boring" |
| 適合場景 Suitable for | 已有理論基礎<br>Existing theoretical basis | 未知領域、emerging issues<br>Unknown domains, emerging issues |

**最佳策略：兩者結合 / Best strategy: combine both**

- 主要假設驅動
  Main component is hypothesis-driven
- 同時保留 20% 探索性空間
  Reserve 20% for exploration
- 若主要假設被否證，轉向探索性解釋
  If main hypothesis falsified, pivot to exploratory explanation

### 2.2 假設的可證偽性 (Falsifiability)
### Falsifiability (Popper)

Popper 標準：一個科學假設必須**原則上可被證偽**。  
Popper's criterion: a scientific hypothesis must be **in principle falsifiable**.

**好的假設 / Good hypothesis:**

- 「此處地下水 pH 與鐵濃度呈負相關，相關係數 < -0.5」
  "Groundwater pH here is negatively correlated with Fe concentration, r < -0.5"
- 可被測量、可被反例推翻
  Measurable, refutable by counter-example

**不好的假設 / Bad hypothesis:**

- 「此處的水化學很複雜」
  "Water chemistry here is complex"
- 無法測量、無法反證
  Not measurable, not refutable

**無法證偽的「假設」 = 信仰，不應出現在科學論文中**  
**Unfalsifiable "hypotheses" = beliefs, shouldn't appear in scientific papers**

### 2.3 預先登記 (Pre-registration)
### Pre-registration

**為什麼要預登記 / Why pre-register:**

- 防止「HARKing」(Hypothesizing After Results are Known)
  Prevent HARKing
- 防止 p-hacking（重複試驗直到顯著）
  Prevent p-hacking
- 強制思考清楚才開始
  Forces clear thinking before starting

**如何預登記 / How to pre-register:**

- OSF (Open Science Framework) registries
- ClinicalTrials.gov（醫學）
- 各領域專用 registry
- 至少提交研究計畫書到倫理委員會

**對田野的含意 / Implications for fieldwork:**

- 在出發前寫好計畫書
  Write plan before departure
- 標明所有假設、預期樣本數、統計方法
  Document all hypotheses, expected sample size, statistical methods
- 出發後仍可改，但必須記錄為「protocol deviation」
  Can change after, but must document as "protocol deviation"

### 2.4 Power 分析 (Statistical Power)
### Statistical Power Analysis

**為什麼需要 power 分析 / Why needed:**

- 若樣本太少，永遠得不到顯著結果
  If sample too small, never get significant result
- 若樣本太多，浪費資源
  If sample too large, waste resources

**計算 / Calculation:**

給定效果量 (effect size) $d$、顯著水準 $\alpha$、期望 power $1-\beta$，求最小 $n$。  
Given effect size $d$, significance $\alpha$, power $1-\beta$, find minimum $n$.

常用工具 / Common tools: G*Power, R `pwr` package

**對田野設計的含意 / Implications for field design:**

- 採樣點數 + 採樣次數 + 實驗室重複數 → 全部需考慮
  Sampling sites × times × lab replicates → all must be considered
- 經驗：環境研究常見 power 不足（樣本太少）
  Experience: environmental studies often underpowered

### 2.5 專家如何使用這個模型
### How Experts Use This Mental Model

- **出發前寫好假設清單**（每個觀測對應一個假設）
  Before departure, write hypothesis list (each observation ↔ hypothesis)
- **為否定結果保留空間**：否定也是結果
  Reserve space for null results: null is also a result
- **區分「探索性」（標明）vs「假設驅動」（嚴格）」
  Distinguish "exploratory" (flag it) vs "hypothesis-driven" (rigorous)
- **同行評審時檢查「是否真的檢驗了假設」**
  In peer review, check "did they really test the hypothesis"

### 2.6 深度自測問題
### Deep Self-Test Questions

1. 為什麼「HARKing」對科學有害？即使研究者是誠實的？  
   Why is HARKing harmful to science, even when researchers are honest?
2. 如何區分「探索性發現」和「HARKing」？  
   How do you distinguish "exploratory finding" from "HARKing"?
3. 在兩週的田野工作中，如何平衡假設驅動與探索性？  
   In a two-week field campaign, how do you balance hypothesis-driven and exploratory work?
4. 為什麼環境研究常見 power 不足？這對結論的可靠性意味著什麼？  
   Why are environmental studies often underpowered? What does this mean for conclusion reliability?

### 核心結論 / Core Takeaway

> **沒有假設的數據是數字，不是科學。每個觀測都應回答一個具體問題。**  
> **Data without hypotheses are numbers, not science. Every observation should answer a specific question.**

## 3. 物流、安全與倫理是科學的一部分
## Logistics, Safety, and Ethics Are Part of Science

**Access, permits, community relations, and personal safety determine what science is possible.**

「不科學」的不是無法做實驗，而是把物流、安全、倫理視為「不屬於科學家的工作」。優秀的田野科學家必須同時是後勤、外交官、安全官。  
It's not "unscientific" that you can't do experiments — it's the failure to treat logistics, safety, and ethics as "not the scientist's job." Excellent field scientists must simultaneously be logistics coordinators, diplomats, and safety officers.

### 3.1 三類「非技術」維度
### Three "non-technical" dimensions

**(a) 物流 / Logistics:**

- 進入 Access: 路權、土地權、邊界、領空
  Access: right of way, land rights, borders, airspace
- 設備 Equipment: 電源、運輸、抗震、抗溫
  Equipment: power, transport, shock, temperature
- 通訊 Communication: 衛星電話、無線電、網路
  Communication: sat phone, radio, internet
- 後勤 Supply: 水、食物、燃料、零件
  Supply: water, food, fuel, parts
- 時程 Timing: 季節、天氣窗口、潮汐
  Timing: season, weather window, tide

**(b) 安全 / Safety:**

- 物理 Physical: 地形、氣候、野生動物、設備故障
  Physical: terrain, weather, wildlife, equipment failure
- 化學 Chemical: 毒性氣體、腐蝕性物質、污染物
  Chemical: toxic gases, corrosives, pollutants
- 生物 Biological: 病原體、過敏、寄生蟲
  Biological: pathogens, allergies, parasites
- 心理 Psychological: 隔離、衝突、壓力
  Psychological: isolation, conflict, stress
- 法規 Regulatory: 工作時數、保險、緊急聯絡
  Regulatory: work hours, insurance, emergency contact

**(c) 倫理 / Ethics:**

- 研究倫理: IRB 批准、同意書、未成年人、敏感族群
  Research ethics: IRB approval, consent, minors, vulnerable groups
- 環境倫理: 對研究對象的影響（不要破壞你研究的系統）
  Environmental ethics: impact on study system (don't destroy what you study)
- 數據倫理: 數據共享、歸屬、原住民知識
  Data ethics: sharing, attribution, indigenous knowledge
- 文化倫理: 尊重在地傳統、儀式、隱私
  Cultural ethics: respect local tradition, ceremony, privacy
- 利益倫理: 利益衝突、贊助者
  Benefit ethics: conflict of interest, sponsors

### 3.2 許可 (Permits) 的多層次
### The Multiple Layers of Permits

1. **機構 IRB / IACUC**（機構審查委員會）
   Institutional IRB / IACUC
2. **研究許可**（國家公園、保護區、海洋）
   Research permits (national parks, reserves, marine)
3. **出口/進口許可**（樣品、生物、化學品）
   Export/import permits (samples, biological, chemicals)
4. **土地使用許可**（私有土地、部落）
   Land use permits (private, tribal)
5. **原住民 / 社區同意 (FPIC)**
   Indigenous / community consent (FPIC)
6. **危險物品運輸**
   Hazardous material transport
7. **保險**
   Insurance
8. **簽證 / 工作許可**
   Visa / work permit

**FPIC (Free, Prior and Informed Consent) 特別重要 / FPIC especially important:**

- 自由：不受脅迫
  Free: not coerced
- 事前：研究開始前
  Prior: before research begins
- 知情：完全理解研究目的
  Informed: fully understand purpose

### 3.3 跨文化田野的倫理
### Cross-Cultural Field Ethics

**原則 / Principles:**

- **謙遜 / Humility:** 我是客人
  I am a guest
- **學習 / Learning:** 從在地知識中學習
  Learn from local knowledge
- **共享 / Sharing:** 數據、結果應回到社群
  Data and results should return to the community
- **避免傷害 / Avoid harm:** 避免文化標籤化、刻板印象
  Avoid cultural labeling, stereotyping
- **建立長期關係 / Long-term relationship:** 不是 extractive research
  Not extractive research

**具體做法 / Concrete practices:**

- 用當地語言（即使翻譯）
  Use local language (even with translation)
- 在地助手（field assistant）作為文化橋樑
  Local assistant as cultural bridge
- 工作前舉行介紹會議
  Hold introduction meeting before work
- 工作後感謝、回報
  Thank and report back after

### 3.4 風險評估矩陣
### Risk Assessment Matrix

| | 低機率 Low prob. | 中機率 Med. prob. | 高機率 High prob. |
|---|---|---|---|
| **高影響 High impact** | 中 Med. | 高 High | 極高 Extreme |
| **中影響 Med. impact** | 低 Low | 中 Med. | 高 High |
| **低影響 Low impact** | 低 Low | 低 Low | 中 Med. |

**應對 / Response:**

- 高/極高：需要備案、應急計畫、保險
  High/Extreme: backup, contingency, insurance required
- 中：標準程序 + 意識
  Medium: standard procedure + awareness
- 低：常規運行
  Low: routine operations

### 3.5 專家如何使用這個模型
### How Experts Use This Mental Model

- **物流清單要提前 3 個月準備**
  Logistics list prepared 3 months ahead
- **安全簡報每天早上舉行**
  Safety briefing every morning
- **倫理不只是 IRB——是全程思維**
  Ethics isn't just IRB — it's a throughout mindset
- **尊重在地 = 更好的科學**：在地知識常節省數月工作
  Respecting locals = better science: local knowledge often saves months of work

### 3.6 深度自測問題
### Deep Self-Test Questions

1. 為什麼 FPIC 在環境研究中特別重要（不只是醫學研究）？  
   Why is FPIC especially important in environmental research (not just medical)?
2. 在邊遠地區，沒有手機信號時應如何設計通訊與安全協議？  
   In remote areas without cell signal, how should communication and safety protocols be designed?
3. 如何處理「在地知識 vs 西方科學」之間的衝突？  
   How do you handle conflicts between "local knowledge" and "Western science"?
4. 一次 3 週的田野工作中，最常被忽視的安全風險是什麼？  
   In a 3-week field campaign, what is the most commonly overlooked safety risk?

### 核心結論 / Core Takeaway

> **田野科學是「科學 × 後勤 × 安全 × 倫理」的乘積。任何一個為零，整個為零。**  
> **Field science is the product of "science × logistics × safety × ethics." Any factor being zero zeroes out the whole.**

## 4. 資料品質比資料數量更重要
## Data Quality Is More Important Than Data Quantity

**A few well-documented, high-quality measurements outweigh large volumes of poorly contextualized data.**

現代感測器讓我們每秒收集數千個數據點，但「資料洪流」不等於「資訊」。在環境研究中，**正確記錄的 10 個樣本比錯記的 10000 個更有科學價值**。  
Modern sensors let us collect thousands of data points per second, but "data deluge" ≠ "information." In environmental research, **10 well-documented samples are more scientifically valuable than 10,000 poorly contextualized ones**.

### 4.1 資料品質的「V 模型」/ Data Quality "V Model"

```
原始信號 Raw signal
  ↓ 校正 Calibration
校正值 Calibrated value
  ↓ 品管 QC
驗證值 Validated value
  ↓ 元數據 Metadata
可解釋值 Interpretable value
  ↓ 整合 Integration
資訊 Information
  ↓ 決策 Decision
行動 Action
```

每個步驟都可能引入或消除錯誤。  
Every step can introduce or eliminate errors.

### 4.2 現場 QA/QC 框架
### Field QA/QC Framework

**校正 / Calibration:**

- 校正頻率: 每日（理想）到每週（最低）
  Frequency: daily (ideal) to weekly (minimum)
- 多點校正（5 點以上）
  Multi-point (≥ 5 points)
- 校正標準必須追溯到 NIST 或同等標準
  Standards traceable to NIST or equivalent

**重複 / Replication:**

- 現場重複: 同樣條件下重複採樣
  Field duplicate: repeat sampling under same conditions
- 實驗室重複: 同樣樣品多次分析
  Lab duplicate: same sample analyzed multiple times
- 目標 RSD < 10%（環境樣品常 < 20%）
  Target RSD < 10% (environmental samples often < 20%)

**空白 / Blanks:**

- 現場空白 Field blank: 純水在現場打開又密封
  Open and close pure water in the field
- 旅行空白 Travel blank: 從實驗室到現場再返回
  Lab to field and back, unopened
- 設備空白 Equipment blank: 純水通過設備
  Pure water through equipment

**加標回收 / Spike recovery:**

- 在樣品中加入已知濃度，測回收率
  Add known concentration to sample, measure recovery
- 接受範圍: 70–130%（視樣品類型）
  Acceptable: 70–130% (depends on sample type)

**標準參考物質 (SRM) / Standard Reference Material:**

- 定期分析 SRM 確認準確度
  Periodically analyze SRM to confirm accuracy
- 偏差 > 5% → 檢查方法
  Bias > 5% → check method

### 4.3 現場記錄的「五個 W」
### The "Five W's" of Field Documentation

每個觀測必須記錄：
Every observation must record:

- **WHO:** 誰採的樣、誰測的
  Who: who sampled, who measured
- **WHAT:** 採了什麼、測了什麼、方法
  What: what was sampled/measured, method
- **WHEN:** 精確時間（含時區）
  When: precise time (with time zone)
- **WHERE:** GPS 座標、深度、高度
  Where: GPS, depth, elevation
- **HOW:** 採樣協議、儀器、條件
  How: sampling protocol, instrument, conditions

**WHY 也重要 / WHY also matters:**

- 為什麼在這裡採這個樣？
  Why this sample at this location?
- 假設是什麼？
  What is the hypothesis?

### 4.4 元數據的標準
### Metadata Standards

**ISO 19115 (地理資訊元數據):**
- 標準化元數據結構
  Standardized metadata structure
- 包含品質、來源、限制
  Includes quality, source, constraints

**FAIR 原則 / FAIR Principles:**

- **Findable:** 可被發現
- **Accessible:** 可被取用
- **Interoperable:** 可被整合
- **Reusable:** 可被重複使用

**在 ICE Professional Review 中 / For ICE Professional Review:**

- 元數據完整是「Attribute 4: Technical Ability」的證據
  Complete metadata is evidence for "Attribute 4: Technical Ability"

### 4.5 感測器漂移與校準
### Sensor Drift and Calibration

**為什麼漂移 / Why drift:**

- 老化 Aging
- 污染 Fouling
- 化學侵蝕 Chemical attack
- 機械磨損 Mechanical wear
- 環境應力 Environmental stress

**漂移的後果 / Consequences of drift:**

- 趨勢被誤判
  Trend misjudged
- 絕對值錯誤
  Absolute value wrong
- 跨站可比性破壞
  Cross-site comparability broken

**校正策略 / Calibration strategy:**

- 雙點校正（零點 + 跨距）
  Two-point (zero + span)
- 多點校正（含中間）
  Multi-point (with mid-range)
- 現場標準 (in-situ standard)
- 自動校正（內建）
  Auto-calibration (built-in)

### 4.6 專家如何使用這個模型
### How Experts Use This Mental Model

- **在出發前準備好所有 QA/QC 物資**
  Prepare all QA/QC supplies before departure
- **每天記錄日誌**（即使「無聊」的日子）
  Daily log (even "boring" days)
- **數據進入資料庫前必須先 metadata 完整**
  Before data enters database, metadata must be complete
- **報告中永遠給出品質指標**（LOD, RSD, 回收率）
  Always report quality indicators (LOD, RSD, recovery)

### 4.7 深度自測問題
### Deep Self-Test Questions

1. 為什麼現場空白比實驗室空白更重要？  
   Why are field blanks more important than lab blanks?

3. 為什麼「不可量化不確定性」比「可量化不確定性」更危險？  
   Why is "non-quantifiable uncertainty" more dangerous than "quantifiable uncertainty"?
4. 在資源有限時，你會優先做哪些 QA/QC 步驟？  
   Under resource constraints, which QA/QC steps do you prioritize?

### 核心結論 / Core Takeaway

> **無元數據的數據 = 廢紙。10 個有完整元數據的樣本 > 10000 個無元數據的數據。**  
> **Data without metadata = wastepaper. 10 well-documented samples > 10,000 undocumented data points.**

## 5. 跨學科與在地知識必須整合
## Interdisciplinary and Local Knowledge Must Be Integrated

**Local stakeholders and other disciplines often hold critical information that pure technical approaches miss.**

這是 TREX 的核心精神，也是當代環境研究的範式轉移：從「科學家告訴在地人怎麼辦」到「與在地人共同創造知識」。  
This is the core spirit of TREX and the contemporary paradigm shift in environmental research: from "scientist tells locals what to do" to "co-creation of knowledge with locals."

### 5.1 為什麼需要在地知識 / Why local knowledge is needed

**在地知識的類型 / Types of local knowledge:**

- **觀察型 / Observational:** 「這條河每年三月氾濫」
  "This river floods every March"
- **解釋型 / Explanatory:** 「氾濫時魚群會游到上游迴水區」
  "When flooding, fish move to upstream backwater"
- **規範型 / Normative:** 「這是神聖水源，不能開發」
  "This is sacred water, cannot develop"
- **實踐型 / Practical:** 「用這種草可以治魚病」
  "Use this herb to cure fish disease"

**對科學的價值 / Value to science:**

- 提供長期觀察（科學家難以達成的時間尺度）
  Long-term observations (time scales scientists cannot match)
- 填補資料空白
  Fill data gaps
- 提供假設（在地人可能早知曉規律）
  Provide hypotheses (locals may know patterns earlier)
- 設計更貼地的方案
  Design more grounded solutions

**對在地的價值 / Value to local community:**

- 科學驗證在地智慧
  Scientific validation of local wisdom
- 引入新方法、技術
  Introduction of new methods, technologies
- 連接政策、資源
  Connect to policy, resources

### 5.2 跨學科整合的具體做法
### Concrete Practices for Interdisciplinary Integration

**a. 團隊組成 / Team composition:**

- 環境工程師（物理化學）
  Environmental engineer (physicochemical)
- 生態學家（生物）
  Ecologist (biological)
- 社會學家 / 人類學家（在地）
  Sociologist / anthropologist (local)
- 經濟學家（成本效益）
  Economist (cost-benefit)
- 在地代表 / 利益相關者
  Local representative / stakeholders

**b. 共同設計 (Co-design) / Co-design:**

- 從問題定義開始就納入在地
  Local stakeholders involved from problem definition
- 不是「先做再展示」
  Not "do first, present later"
- 而是「共同發現問題」
  But "co-discover the problem"

**c. 跨學科語言 / Interdisciplinary language:**

- 工程師學會聽在地語言
  Engineers learn to listen in local language
- 生態學家學會用在地比喻
  Ecologists learn local metaphors
- 社會學家學會用技術指標
  Sociologists learn technical metrics

### 5.3 案例：Co-management 漁業
### Case: Co-managed Fisheries

- 漁業資源管理傳統上由政府單獨管
  Traditionally managed by government alone
- 失敗模式: 漁民不遵守、知識未利用
  Failure: fishers don't comply, knowledge unused
- 成功模式: 漁民 + 政府 + 科學家共同決策
  Success model: fishers + government + scientists co-decide
- 結果: 漁業可持續，漁民支持
  Result: sustainable fishery, fisher support

### 5.4 跨文化研究中的權力不對等
### Power Asymmetries in Cross-Cultural Research

**問題 / Problems:**

- 西方科學家 vs 在地居民 (語言、資金、權力)
  Western scientist vs local resident (language, funding, power)
- 學術評價體系偏重英文期刊
  Academic evaluation favors English journals
- 在地知識常被視為「民俗」而非知識
  Local knowledge often seen as "folklore" not knowledge

**解方 / Solutions:**

- 資料回饋社群
  Data feedback to community
- 在地翻譯者
  Local translators
- 長期承諾 (不 extractive)
  Long-term commitment (not extractive)
- 雙向翻譯：將在地概念引入科學詞彙
  Two-way translation: introduce local concepts into scientific vocabulary

## 詳解 1：空間覆蓋 vs 時間解析度
## Solution 1: Spatial Coverage vs Temporal Resolution

**問題 / Question:**  
在設計田野採樣方案時，如何平衡空間覆蓋與時間解析度？  
When designing a field sampling scheme, how do you balance spatial coverage and temporal resolution?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 核心矛盾 / Core dilemma**

固定資源下，採樣預算是有限的：
Under fixed resources, sampling budget is limited:

$$B = n_{sites} \times n_{times} \times n_{replicates} \times cost_{per\_sample}$$

- 增加站點 → 空間覆蓋提升，但每站時間解析度下降
  Add sites → spatial coverage ↑, but per-site time resolution ↓
- 增加時間 → 時間解析度提升，但空間覆蓋下降
  Add times → time resolution ↑, but spatial coverage ↓

**2. 決策依據 / Decision basis**

**a. 問題類型 / Problem type:**

| 問題類型 Type | 優先 Priority |
|---|---|
| 空間梯度（汙染源識別）<br>Spatial gradient (source identification) | 空間覆蓋 Spatial coverage |
| 時間動態（事件過程）<br>Temporal dynamics (event process) | 時間解析度 Temporal resolution |
| 平衡型（背景調查）<br>Balanced (background survey) | 兩者平衡 Balance |

**b. 變異性結構 / Variability structure:**

- 若空間變異 >> 時間變異 → 加站點
  If spatial variability >> temporal → add sites
- 若時間變異 >> 空間變異 → 加時間
  If temporal >> spatial → add times

可由 preliminary survey 估計
Can be estimated from preliminary survey

**c. 統計設計 / Statistical design:**

- **Nested design:** 空間主要 + 時間巢狀
  Nested: spatial main, temporal nested
- **Crossed design:** 兩者交叉（最完整，但成本高）
  Crossed: both crossed (most complete, expensive)
- **Stratified:** 分層採樣
  Stratified: stratified sampling

**3. 實際策略 / Practical strategies**

**a. 嵌套設計 / Nested design:**

- 主要站: 5–10 個（高頻）
  Main sites: 5–10 (high frequency)
- 次要站: 20–30 個（中頻）
  Secondary sites: 20–30 (medium frequency)
- 驗證站: 50+ 個（一次性背景）
  Validation sites: 50+ (one-time background)

**b. 移動平台 / Mobile platforms:**

- 若資源允許，用船、車、無人機作「移動站」
  Use boats, cars, drones as "mobile stations"
- 短時間內覆蓋大空間
  Cover large space in short time

**c. 感測器網絡 / Sensor networks:**

- 高密度自動感測器 → 解決時間
  High-density automatic sensors → solve time
- 移動採樣 → 解決空間
  Mobile sampling → solve space

**4. 範例：流域水質調查 / Example: catchment water-quality survey**

- 目標：找出汙染源
  Goal: locate pollution source
- 設計：50 個站點，乾濕季各 1 次
  Design: 50 sites, dry and wet season once each
- 加上：3 個關鍵站連續監測（30 分鐘解析度）
  Add: 3 key sites continuous monitoring (30 min resolution)

**5. 報告要素 / Required report elements**

- 採樣設計的理由
  Justification of sampling design
- 統計 power 分析
  Statistical power analysis
- 限制說明
  Limitations
- 不可達站點的影響評估
  Impact of inaccessible sites

## 詳解 2：空白、重複與標準品
## Solution 2: Blanks, Duplicates, and Standards in Field QC

**問題 / Question:**  
說明為何空白樣、重複樣與標準品在現場品管中不可或缺。  
Explain why blanks, duplicates, and standards are indispensable in field QC.

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 為什麼需要 QC / Why QC is needed**

- 環境樣品濃度常接近 LOD
  Environmental concentrations often near LOD
- 汙染風險（採樣、運輸、儲存）
  Contamination risk (sampling, transport, storage)
- 變異性大
  High variability
- 「Garbage in, garbage out」對環境數據尤其嚴重
  GIGO especially severe for environmental data

**2. 空白樣 / Blanks**

**a. 現場空白 (Field blank):**

- 純水在現場「假採樣」（打開瓶、密封、標記）
  Pure water "fake sampled" in field (open, close, label)
- 檢測：採樣過程中是否引入汙染
  Tests: contamination introduced during sampling
- 例：在農田附近採樣可能引入農藥背景
  Example: sampling near farmland may introduce pesticide background

**b. 旅行空白 (Travel blank):**

- 從實驗室密封帶到現場再返回，**全程不開**
  Sealed lab-to-field-and-back, **never opened**
- 檢測：運輸過程是否引入汙染
  Tests: contamination during transport

**c. 設備空白 (Equipment blank):**

- 純水通過採樣設備
  Pure water through sampling equipment
- 檢測：設備是否為汙染源
  Tests: whether equipment is contamination source

**3. 重複樣 / Duplicates**

**a. 現場重複 (Field duplicate):**

- 同一條件下採兩瓶
  Two bottles sampled under same condition
- 計算 RSD% 評估**採樣精密度**
  RSD% assesses **sampling precision**
- 接受 RSD < 20% (環境樣品常見)
  Accept RSD < 20% (typical for environmental)

**b. 實驗室重複 (Lab duplicate):**

- 同一樣品分析多次
  Same sample analyzed multiple times
- 評估**分析精密度**
  Assesses **analytical precision**
- 接受 RSD < 10% (儀器)
  Accept RSD < 10% (instrument)

**為什麼需要兩種 / Why both needed:**

- 現場重複 = 採樣誤差（最大）
  Field duplicate = sampling error (largest)
- 實驗室重複 = 分析誤差
  Lab duplicate = analytical error
- 總誤差 = 採樣 + 分析（不可忽視任一）
  Total = sampling + analysis (neither negligible)

**4. 標準品 / Standards**

**a. 校正標準 (Calibration standard):**

- 已知濃度，用於建立校正曲線
  Known concentration for calibration curve
- 多點（5+ 點）覆蓋預期範圍
  Multi-point (5+ points) over expected range
- **追溯到 NIST 或同等**
  Traceable to NIST or equivalent

**b. 加標回收 (Spike recovery):**

- 樣品 + 已知標準 → 測回收率
  Sample + known standard → measure recovery
- 接受: 70–130% (環境樣品)
  Accept: 70–130% (environmental)
- 評估**基質效應**
  Assesses **matrix effects**

**c. 標準參考物質 (SRM):**

- 已知濃度的「標準樣品」
  "Standard sample" with known concentration
- 用於確認**準確度**
  Confirms **accuracy**
- 來源：NIST、IRMM、或其他認證機構
  Source: NIST, IRMM, or other certified body

**5. 頻率建議 / Frequency recommendations**

| QC 類型 Type | 頻率 Frequency |
|---|---|
| 現場空白 Field blank | 每 10–20 樣本 1 個<br>1 per 10–20 samples |
| 旅行空白 Travel blank | 每批 1 個<br>1 per batch |
| 設備空白 Equipment blank | 每設備/每天 1 個<br>1 per equipment per day |
| 現場重複 Field duplicate | 每 10–20 樣本 1 對<br>1 pair per 10–20 samples |
| 加標回收 Spike | 每 10–20 樣本 1 個<br>1 per 10–20 samples |
| SRM | 每批 1 個<br>1 per batch |
| 校正 Calibration | 每日 (Daily) |

**6. 數據有效性判斷 / Data validity judgment**

若 QC 失敗：
If QC fails:

- 樣品重測 Re-analyze
- 設備校正 Recalibrate
- 標記數據 Flag data
- 報告影響 Report impact
- 不可隱瞞 Don't hide

**7. 報告中的呈現 / Reporting**

必須呈現：
Must present:

- QC 種類與頻率
  QC type and frequency
- QC 結果 (RSD, 回收率, 空白值)
  QC results
- 接受標準
  Acceptance criteria
- 失敗時的處理
  Action on failure

## 詳解 3：不可達站點的處理
## Solution 3: Handling Inaccessible Sites

**問題 / Question:**  
如何處理「無法到達」或「許可被拒」的測站？對結論有何影響？  
How do you handle "inaccessible" or "permit-denied" sites? What is the impact on conclusions?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 為什麼不可達 / Why inaccessible**

- **物理:** 地形險峻、距離遙遠、無路
  Physical: rugged terrain, distance, no road
- **法律:** 私有土地、保護區、領海
  Legal: private, protected area, territorial water
- **政治:** 衝突區、敏感設施
  Political: conflict, sensitive facility
- **社會:** 在地不歡迎、健康風險
  Social: local opposition, health risk
- **氣候:** 季節無法進入
  Climate: season inaccessible

**2. 處理策略 / Handling strategies**

**a. 替代採樣 / Alternative sampling:**

- 鄰近可達站點 (Nearby accessible sites)
- 遙測 (Remote sensing): 衛星、無人機
  Remote sensing: satellite, drone
- 公民科學 (Citizen science)
- 自動浮標 (Autonomous buoys)

**b. 模型推估 / Model extrapolation:**

- 空間插值 (Kriging, IDW)
  Spatial interpolation

## 詳解 5：兩週內可檢驗假設
## Solution 5: Falsifiable Hypothesis in 2 Weeks

**問題 / Question:**  
給定一個環境問題，如何將其轉化為可在兩週內完成的可檢驗假設？  
Given an environmental problem, how do you transform it into a testable hypothesis that can be completed in 2 weeks?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 從大問題到小假設 / From big problem to small hypothesis**

**範例：湖泊優養化**
Example: lake eutrophication

- 大問題：為什麼湖中藻華越來越嚴重？
  Big: why is algal bloom getting worse?
- 中問題：P 輸入是否在過去 5 年增加？
  Medium: has P loading increased in past 5 years?
- **小假設：**「該湖主要支流 TP 濃度在 5 年內增加 30% 以上」
  **Small hypothesis: "Main tributary TP concentration has increased >30% in 5 years"**

**2. 假設設計原則 / Hypothesis design principles**

**a. 可測量 (Measurable):**

- 變量必須可量化
  Variable must be quantifiable
- 「越來越嚴重」→「TP 濃度 +30%」

**b. 可證偽 (Falsifiable):**

- 必須原則上可被反例推翻
  Must be refutable in principle
- 「可能增加」不可證偽
  "May increase" is not falsifiable

**c. 兩週可達 (Achievable in 2 weeks):**

- 採樣 → 分析 → 統計 → 結論
  Sampling → analysis → statistics → conclusion
- 預算、人力、時間可達
  Within budget, manpower, time

**d. 預期效果量 (Expected effect size):**

- 必須足夠大，使樣本數合理
  Must be large enough that sample size is feasible
- 太小 → 樣本數爆炸
  Too small → sample size explodes
- 太大 → 不現實
  Too large → unrealistic

**3. SMART 假設 / SMART hypothesis**

- **S**pecific 具體：變量、空間、時間明確
  Specific: variable, space, time clear
- **M**easurable 可測量
- **A**chievable 可達成
- **R**elevant 相關：回答大問題
  Relevant: addresses the big question
- **T**ime-bound 有時限

**4. 範例：2 週內可完成的假設集 / Examples: hypotheses doable in 2 weeks**

| 領域 Domain | 假設 Hypothesis | 方法 Method |
|---|---|---|
| 河流污染 River pollution | 「暴雨後下游 SS 比上游高 5 倍」<br>"After storm, downstream SS is 5× upstream" | 暴雨前後採樣 Before/after storm |
| 土壤污染 Soil | 「農田表層 0–10 cm Cd 高於背景 2 倍」<br>"Topsoil 0–10 cm Cd is 2× background" | 採樣 20 點 + 5 背景 |
| 地下水 Groundwater | 「污染源下游 50 m 內 TCE 超過 MCL」<br>"TCE exceeds MCL within 50 m downstream" | 採樣 3 個深度 + 對照 |
| 空氣 Air | 「工業區下風 PM₂.₅ 高於上風 50%」<br>"Downwind PM₂.₅ is 50% higher than upwind" | 上風/下風站 Upwind/downwind sites |
| 生態 Ecology | 「城市公園鳥類多樣性低於郊野 30%」<br>"Urban park bird diversity is 30% less than rural" | 點計調查 Point counts |

**5. 假設設計工作流 / Hypothesis design workflow**

```
1. 從大問題開始 Start with big problem
2. 文獻回顧找現有證據 Literature review
3. 識別關鍵變量 Identify key variables
4. 估算預期效果量 Estimate expected effect size
5. 計算所需樣本數（power analysis）Sample size (power)
6. 設計採樣方案 Design sampling
7. 預登記 Pre-register
8. 估算時間與資源 Estimate time and resources
9. 確認 2 週內可行 Confirm 2-week feasibility
10. 開始執行 Execute
```

**6. 預登記的價值 / Value of pre-registration**

- 即使在兩週田野也應該做
  Should do even for 2-week field
- 簡單 OSF submission 即可
  Simple OSF submission is enough
- 防止 deadline-driven shortcuts
  Prevents deadline-driven shortcuts

## 詳解 6：感測器校正 vs 實驗室分析誤差
## Solution 6: Sensor Calibration vs Lab Analysis Error

**問題 / Question:**  
說明現場感測器校正與實驗室分析之間的誤差傳遞。  
Explain error propagation between field sensor calibration and lab analysis.

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 兩種測量鏈 / Two measurement chains**

**a. 現場感測器鏈 / Field sensor chain:**

環境 → 感測器響應 → 模擬/數位 → 校正 → 數據
Environment → sensor response → A/D → calibration → data

誤差來源 / Error sources:
- 儀器誤差 Instrument error
- 漂移 Drift
- 環境效應 (T, P, 干擾物) Environmental effects (T, P, interferences)
- 校正誤差 Calibration error

**b. 實驗室分析鏈 / Lab analysis chain:**

樣品 → 前處理 → 儀器分析 → 校正 → 結果
Sample → pretreatment → instrument → calibration → result

誤差來源 / Error sources:
- 採樣誤差 Sampling error
- 儲存誤差 Storage error
- 前處理誤差 Pretreatment error
- 儀器誤差 Instrument error
- 基質效應 Matrix effects

**2. 誤差傳遞公式 / Error propagation formulas**

**a. 加法誤差 (Additive):** $\sigma_f^2 = \sum_i \sigma_i^2$

**b. 乘法誤差 (Multiplicative):** $\frac{\sigma_f^2}{f^2} = \sum_i \left(\frac{\sigma_i}{x_i}\right)^2$

**c. 函數誤差:** $\sigma_f^2 = \sum_i \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}\right)^2 \sigma_i^2$

**3. 典型誤差量級 / Typical error magnitudes**

| 測量 Measurement | 感測器 Sensor | 實驗室 Lab | 差距 Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| pH | ±0.1 | ±0.05 | 2× |
| DO 溶氧 | ±0.5 mg/L | ±0.2 mg/L | 2.5× |
| 電導度 Conductivity | ±5% | ±1% | 5× |
| 濁度 Turbidity | ±10% | ±5% | 2× |
| 營養鹽 Nutrient | — | ±5–10% | (lab only) |
| 重金屬 Heavy metals | — | ±10–20% | (lab only) |
| VOC | ±25% | ±15% | ~1.5× |
| TOC | — | ±5% | (lab only) |

**4. 校正的核心問題 / Core issues in calibration**

**a. 校正頻率 / Calibration frequency:**

- 太頻繁：浪費時間
  Too often: wastes time
- 太稀疏：漂移未發現
  Too sparse: drift unnoticed
- 經驗：每日 (Daily) + 重大事件後
  Empirical: daily + after major events

**b. 校正標準的追溯 / Standard traceability:**

- 必須追溯到 NIST 或同等
  Traceable to NIST or equivalent
- 否則不同實驗室結果不可比
  Otherwise cross-lab results not comparable
- 對 ICE 證據尤其重要
  Especially important for ICE evidence

**c. 現場校正 vs 實驗室校正的差異 / Field vs lab calibration:**

- 現場：基質複雜、溫度變化、干擾物
  Field: complex matrix, T variation, interferences
- 實驗室：控制條件、純標準
  Lab: controlled conditions, pure standards
- 現場校正結果可能與實驗室**系統性偏差**
  Field calibration may have **systematic bias** vs lab

**5. 誤差控制的最佳實踐 / Best practices for error control**

**a. 跨驗證 (Cross-validation):**

- 同一樣品用兩種方法測
  Same sample with two methods
- 例：感測器 pH + 實驗室 pH 計
  Example: sensor pH + lab pH meter
- 計算偏差與相關性
  Calculate bias and correlation

**b. 標準加入法 (Standard addition):**

- 在樣品中加入已知濃度
  Add known concentration to sample
- 測回收率
  Measure recovery
- 校正基質效應
  Correct for matrix effects

**c. 多點校正 (Multi-point calibration):**

- 5+ 點覆蓋預期範圍
  5+ points over expected range
- 包含零點和滿量程
  Include zero and full scale
- 定期驗證 (Check standard)
  Periodic verification

**6. 報告中的誤差呈現 / Error presentation in reports**

必須呈現 / Must present:

- 各步驟的誤差來源
  Error sources at each step
- 總誤差估算
  Total error estimate
- 校正曲線方程與 R²
  Calibration equation and R²
- 漂移隨時間的趨勢
  Drift trend over time
- 跨驗證結果
  Cross-validation results

## 詳解 7：氣象與水文同步記錄
## Solution 7: Synchronicity of Ancillary Data

**問題 / Question:**  
為什麼氣象與水文條件的同步記錄對解釋環境數據至關重要？  
Why is synchronous recording of meteorological and hydrological conditions critical for interpreting environmental data?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 環境數據的「上下文依賴」/ "Context dependency" of environmental data**

幾乎所有環境變量的解釋都需要氣象 + 水文上下文：  
Almost all environmental variables need meteorological + hydrological context for interpretation:

- 雨後 vs 雨前樣品
  Post-rain vs pre-rain sample
- 漲潮 vs 退潮
  High tide vs low tide
- 風暴 vs 晴天
  Storm vs clear weather
- 灌溉季 vs 非灌溉季
  Irrigation vs non-irrigation season

**2. 為什麼需要同步 / Why synchronous**

**a. 因果辨識 / Causal identification:**

- 環境數據通常是**反應**，不是原因
  Environmental data usually **response**, not cause
- 沒有驅動數據（氣象、水文）= 無法區分原因 vs 巧合
  Without driver data (meteo, hydro) = cannot distinguish cause vs coincidence

**b. 異常值解釋 / Outlier explanation:**

- 異常高 SS？可能因為昨夜暴雨
  Unusually high SS? Possibly due to last night's storm
- 異常低 DO？可能因為漲潮帶入冷水
  Unusually low DO? Possibly due to cold-water tidal inflow
- 沒有同步記錄 = 異常值變成「神秘數據」
  Without sync = outlier becomes "mysterious data"

**c. 模型校準 / Model calibration:**

- 環境模型需要強迫數據 (forcing data)
  Environmental models need forcing data
- 缺少強迫 = 模型無法運行
  Without forcing = model cannot run

**3. 必備的同步記錄 / Essential synchronous records**

**a. 氣象 / Meteorological:**

- 降雨量 Rainfall (mm/hr 或 daily)
- 氣溫 Temperature
- 風速、風向 Wind speed, direction
- 太陽輻射 Solar radiation
- 濕度、蒸汽壓 Humidity, vapor pressure
- 大氣壓 Atmospheric pressure

**b. 水文 / Hydrological:**

- 流量 Discharge / flow rate
- 水位 Water level
- 潮位（沿海）Tidal level (coastal)
- 波浪（沿海）Wave (coastal)
- 地下水位 Groundwater level

**c. 其他 / Other:**

- 土地利用變化（衛星）
  Land use change (satellite)
- 排放記錄（工業）
  Discharge records (industrial)
- 閘門開度（水利設施）
  Gate opening (hydraulic structures)

**4. 同步記錄的時間精度 / Time precision**

- 快速變化過程（暴雨逕流）→ 分鐘級
  Fast processes (storm runoff) → minute-level
- 日變化（光合作用）→ 小時級
  Diurnal (photosynthesis) → hour-level
- 季節變化 → 日級
  Seasonal → day-level

**5. 數據整合 / Data integration**

**a. 時間對齊 / Time alignment:**

- 統一時區（建議 UTC）
  Unified time zone (recommend UTC)
- 統一時間戳格式（ISO 8601）
  Unified timestamp format (ISO 8601)
- 處理時鐘漂移
  Handle clock drift

**b. 空間對齊 / Spatial alignment:**

- 採樣點與最近氣象站的距離
  Sampling site distance to nearest met station
- 採樣點與水文站的關係
  Site-to-gauge relationship
- 空間代表性
  Spatial representativeness

**6. 案例：暴雨逕流事件 / Case: storm runoff event**

- 環境數據：河流 SS 從 50 增至 500 mg/L
  Environmental: river SS jumped from 50 to 500 mg/L
- 同步數據：昨夜降雨 30 mm；流量從 5 增至 50 m³/s
  Sync: 30 mm rain last night; discharge 5 → 50 m³/s
- **結論：** SS 上升是暴雨逕流的正常響應，**非異常**
  **Conclusion: SS rise is normal storm response, NOT anomaly**

若無同步數據，SS 上升可能被誤判為污染事故。  
Without sync data, SS rise could be misjudged as pollution accident.

**7. 報告中 / In reports**

必須呈現 / Must present:

- 氣象、水文數據來源與時間精度
  Source and time precision of meteo/hydro data
- 採樣時的天氣、水文條件
  Weather, hydro conditions at sampling
- 異常數據的可能解釋（依同步數據）
  Possible explanations of outliers (based on sync data)
- 數據缺漏的影響
  Impact of data gaps

## 詳解 8：代理指標 (Proxy) 的使用
## Solution 8: Using Proxy Indicators

**問題 / Question:**  
在資料稀缺地區，如何用代理指標（proxy）延伸結論的可信度？  
In data-scarce regions, how do you extend the credibility of conclusions using proxy indicators?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 為什麼需要 proxy / Why proxies**

- 直接測量不可行：成本、技術、政治
  Direct measurement infeasible: cost, technology, politics
- 替代測量 (proxy) 可提供間接證據
  Alternative measurements (proxy) provide indirect evidence

**2. Proxy 的類型 / Types of proxies**

**a. 環境 proxy / Environmental proxies:**

- 沉積物記錄 → 歷史污染
  Sediment record → historical pollution
- 樹輪 → 過去氣候
  Tree rings → past climate
- 珊瑚骨骼 → 海洋溫度歷史
  Coral skeleton → ocean temperature history
- 冰芯 → 大氣歷史
  Ice cores → atmospheric history
- 化石花粉 → 過去植被
  Fossil pollen → past vegetation

**b. 生物 proxy / Biological proxies:**

- 指標種 → 水質
  Indicator species → water quality
- 生物多樣性 → 生態健康
  Biodiversity → ecological health
- 生物累積 → 污染物
  Bioaccumulation → pollutants
- 病變率 → 環境壓力
  Disease rate → environmental stress

**c. 社會 proxy / Social proxies:**

- 人口密度 → 人為壓力
  Population density → anthropogenic pressure
- 夜間燈光 → 經濟活動
  Nighttime light → economic activity
- 衛生條件 → 健康風險
  Sanitation → health risk
- 飲用水類型 → 水安全
  Water source type → water security

**d. 遙測 proxy / Remote sensing proxies:**

- NDVI → 植被健康
- LST → 表面溫度
- Chlorophyll-a → 水華
- TSS → 懸浮固體
- 海面高度 → 動力學

**3. Proxy 的有效性 / Proxy validity**

**a. 校準 (Calibration):**

- Proxy 與目標變量必須有已知關係
  Proxy-target relationship must be known
- 區域性校準 (regional calibration)
- 不能直接外推
  Cannot directly extrapolate

**b. 不確定性 / Uncertainty:**

- Proxy 本身有不確定性
  Proxy itself has uncertainty
- 必須量化 (Quantify)
- 通常比直接測量更不確定
  Usually less certain than direct measurement

**c. 獨立性 / Independence:**

- 最好用多個獨立 proxy 交叉驗證
  Best to use multiple independent proxies for cross-validation
- 避免「共因」偏差
  Avoid "common cause" bias

**4. 案例：缺乏監測資料的流域 / Case: unmonitored catchment**

問題 / Problem: 無流量監測站

可用 proxy / Available proxies:

- 鄰近流域流量（流域相似性）
  Adjacent catchment flow (similarity)
- 衛星觀測（GRACE, SWOT）
  Satellite (GRACE, SWOT)
- 在地水位觀察（井、河道）
  Local water level observations (wells, channel)
- 沉積物特徵（洪水痕跡）
  Sediment features (flood marks)
- 居民記憶（歷史洪水）
  Resident memory (historical floods)

**5. 案例：古環境重建 / Case: paleo-environmental reconstruction**

- 缺少 100 年前的水質資料
  No water quality data from 100 years ago
- Proxy: 沉積物岩芯
  Proxy: sediment core
- 指標：粒徑分佈（洪水）、重金屬、營養鹽
  Indicators: grain size (floods), heavy metals, nutrients
- 結合放射性定年（²¹⁰Pb, ¹³⁷Cs）
  Combine with radioactive dating

**6. 報告中 / In reports**

必須呈現 / Must present:

- 使用的 proxy 及其理由
  Proxies used and rationale
- Proxy 與目標的關係（量化）
  Proxy-target relationship (quantified)
- Proxy 的不確定性
  Proxy uncertainty
- 多 proxy 交叉驗證結果
  Multi-proxy cross-validation
- 結論的可信度範圍
  Credibility range of conclusions

**7. 對 ICE 證據的含意 / Implications for ICE evidence**

- 在資料稀缺地區工作 = 經常使用 proxy
  Working in data-scarce regions = frequent proxy use
- 展示對 proxy 限制的認識 = 專業能力
  Demonstrating awareness of proxy limits = professional competence
- 對結論的「強斷言」vs「弱斷言」必須區分
  Must distinguish "strong claim" vs "weak claim"

## 詳解 9：田野對模型校準的價值
## Solution 9: Fieldwork Value for Model Calibration

**問題 / Question:**  
如何設計田野工作以最大化對後續模型校準的價值？  
How should fieldwork be designed to maximize its value for subsequent model calibration?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 模型校準對田野數據的需求 / Model calibration data needs**

- **邊界條件 / Boundary conditions:** 上下游、左右岸、頂底
  Upstream/downstream, lateral, top/bottom
- **初始條件 / Initial conditions:** 模擬開始時的狀態
  State at simulation start
- **強迫數據 / Forcing data:** 氣象、水文
  Meteorological, hydrological
- **校準數據 / Calibration data:** 用於調整參數
  For parameter adjustment
- **驗證數據 / Validation data:** 獨立檢驗
  Independent verification

**2. 採樣設計原則 / Sampling design principles**

**a. 包含參數敏感性的空間 / Space covering parameter sensitivity:**

- 不同土壤、土地利用、地形
  Different soil, land use, topography
- 讓模型參數有差異化的「約束」
  Gives differentiated constraints on model parameters

**b. 包含過程的時間動態 / Time dynamics of processes:**

- 暴雨 vs 乾季
  Storm vs dry
- 漲潮 vs 退潮
  Tidal cycle
- 季節 vs 年際
  Seasonal vs inter-annual

**c. 獨立驗證樣本 / Independent validation samples:**

- 部分樣本不用於校準
  Reserve subset not used for calibration
- 用於驗證 (validation)
- 避免過擬合 (overfitting)
  Avoid overfitting

**3. 觀測的「經濟學」/ Economics of observation**

每個觀測都有成本，最佳配置是：
Each observation has a cost; optimal allocation:

$$\max \text{info gain per cost}$$

- 高敏感度變量 + 高頻觀測 = 高資訊
  High-sensitivity variable + high frequency = high info
- 重要邊界 = 高頻
  Critical boundary = high frequency
- 模型盲區 = 重點採樣
  Model blind spots = priority sampling

**4. 設計範例 / Design example**

水質模型校準 / Water-quality model calibration:

- **邊界：** 上下游 1 站（高頻）
  Boundary: 1 upstream + 1 downstream station (high frequency)
- **沿河道：** 3 站（中頻）
  Along river: 3 stations (medium frequency)
- **主要支流：** 4 支流，每支 1 站（中頻）
  Main tributaries: 4 tributaries, 1 each (medium frequency)
- **關鍵源區：** 1 站（高頻 + 強迫數據）
  Key source: 1 station (high frequency + forcing)
- **驗證：** 1 站（獨立）
  Validation: 1 station (independent)

**5. 校準與驗證的分離 / Separation of calibration and validation**

**a. 為什麼要分離 / Why separate:**

- 用同樣的數據校準 + 驗證 = 過擬合
  Calibrate + validate on same data = overfitting
- 模型可以「記住」特定數據
  Model can "memorize" specific data
- 獨立驗證更能反映真實預測能力
  Independent validation better reflects real predictive ability

**b. 實務做法 / Practical implementation:**

- k-fold cross-validation
- 留出驗證集 (Holdout validation)
- 時間分割 (Temporal split): 用前 70% 校準，後 30% 驗證
  Temporal split: calibrate on first 70%, validate on last 30%

**6. 校準指標 / Calibration metrics**

- **NSE** (Nash-Sutcliffe Efficiency): NSE = 1 - Σ(Q_obs - Q_sim)² / Σ(Q_obs - Q_avg)²
- **RMSE** (Root Mean Square Error)
- **PBIAS** (Percent Bias)
- **R²** (Coefficient of determination)

**接受標準 / Acceptance criteria:**

- NSE > 0.5: 可接受 Acceptable
- NSE > 0.65: 良好 Good
- NSE > 0.75: 很好 Very good
- PBIAS < ±15%
- R² > 0.6

**7. 專家如何使用這個模型
### How Experts Use This Mental Model**

- **在出發前與模型師討論觀測需求**
  Before departure, discuss observation needs with modeler
- **優先敏感度高的觀測**
  Prioritize high-sensitivity observations
- **保留部分獨立樣本**
  Reserve independent samples
- **記錄完整的 metadata 以便模型使用**
  Record complete metadata for model use

## 詳解 10：非技術因素的影響
## Solution 10: Impact of Non-Technical Factors

**問題 / Question:**  
回顧一次田野經驗，哪個非技術因素對科學產出影響最大？  
Looking back at a field experience, which non-technical factor had the biggest impact on scientific output?

**詳解 / Detailed Explanation**

**1. 常見的非技術因素 / Common non-technical factors**

| 因素 Factor | 影響 Impact |
|---|---|
| 語言 Language | 溝通、信任 |
| 文化差異 Cultural difference | 信任、數據解釋 |
| 政治氣候 Political climate | 進入、結論發表 |
| 天氣 Weather | 採樣窗口、儀器故障 |
| 健康 Health | 行程、決策 |
| 團隊 Team | 效率、衝突 |
| 資金 Funding | 範圍、儀器 |
| 倫理審查 Ethics review | 延遲、限制 |
| 個人 Personal | 動機、疲勞 |

**2. 影響最大的常見因素 / Most impactful factors**

**a. 語言 / Language (most common):**

- 影響：溝通不清、誤解、樣品汙染
  Impact: miscommunication, misunderstanding, sample contamination
- 案例：在地助手用錯誤溶劑 → 所有樣品重做
  Case: local assistant used wrong solvent → all samples redone
- 解決：聘在地翻譯；多語版本協議
  Solution: hire local translator; multilingual protocol

**b. 信任 / Trust:**

- 在地不信任 → 不讓進場地 → 數據缺失
  Local distrust → won't allow site access → data gaps
- 建立信任需數月 → 短期田野常失敗
  Building trust takes months → short-term field often fails
- 解決：透過中介；長期承諾
  Solution: through intermediaries; long-term commitment

**c. 團隊 / Team dynamics:**

- 衝突 → 決策遲緩 → 錯過窗口
  Conflict → slow decision → miss window
- 文化差異 → 溝通風格衝突
  Cultural differences → communication style conflict
- 解決：行前明確角色；定期回饋
  Solution: clarify roles before trip; regular debrief

**d. 天氣 / Weather:**

- 颱風 → 整週無法採樣
  Typhoon → entire week unsampleable
- 雨季延期 → 季節數據缺失
  Rainy season delay → seasonal data missing
- 解決：彈性預算（多待一週）；備用計劃
  Solution: flexible budget (extra week); backup plan

**e. 行政 / Administrative:**

- 許可遲延 → 整個田野縮短
  Permit delay → entire field shortened
- 海關扣留儀器 → 關鍵測量無法進行
  Customs hold instrument → key measurement impossible
- 解決：提前 3 個月申請；備用儀器
  Solution: apply 3 months ahead; backup instrument

**3. 為什麼非技術因素常被忽視 / Why often overlooked**

- **技術焦點 / Technical focus:** 訓練強調技術
  Training emphasizes technical
- **敘事偏好 / Narrative preference:** 失敗故事常不提
  Failure stories often suppressed
- **功勞分配 / Credit:** 非技術工作難以「歸功」
  Non-technical work hard to credit

**4. 反思練習 / Reflection exercise**

每次田野後寫：
After each field campaign, write:

- 最成功的是什麼（技術 + 非技術）
  What worked best (technical + non-technical)
- 最困難的是什麼（技術 + 非技術）
  What was hardest (technical + non-technical)
- 下次會做什麼不同
  What would you do differently next time
- 學到了什麼非技術教訓
  What non-technical lessons did you learn

**5. 對 ICE 證據 / For ICE evidence**

ICE Attribute 2 (Communication and Leadership) 強調：
ICE Attribute 2 emphasizes:

- 跨文化溝通
  Cross-cultural communication
- 團隊領導
  Team leadership
- 衝突解決
  Conflict resolution
- 利益相關者管理
  Stakeholder management

**這些都來自非技術因素的反思**
These all come from non-technical reflection.

**6. 教訓 / Lessons**

- **「人是最大的變數」** 在田野中特別真實
  **"People are the biggest variable"** especially true in field
- **預算至少 20% 應留給非技術緩衝**
  At least 20% budget reserved for non-technical buffer
- **行前行後的反思 = 與實驗同樣重要的學習**
  Pre- and post-field reflection = learning as important as experiment
- **在 ICE 面試中，非技術經驗往往是差異化點**
  In ICE interview, non-technical experience is often the differentiator

## 總結 / Summary

這十題共同指向五個深層洞見：  
These ten solutions point to five deep insights:

> **1. 實驗室回答「為什麼」，現場回答「怎樣」。兩者結合才完整。**  
> **1. The lab answers "why"; the field answers "how." Both together is complete.**
>
> **2. 沒有假設的數據是數字，不是科學。每個觀測都應回答一個具體問題。**  
> **2. Data without hypotheses are numbers, not science. Every observation should answer a specific question.**
>
> **3. 田野科學是「科學 × 後勤 × 安全 × 倫理」的乘積。任何一個為零，整個為零。**  
> **3. Field science is the product of "science × logistics × safety × ethics." Any factor being zero zeroes out the whole.**
>
> **4. 無元數據的數據 = 廢紙。10 個有完整元數據的樣本 > 10000 個無元數據的數據。**  
> **4. Data without metadata = wastepaper. 10 well-documented samples > 10,000 undocumented data points.**
>
> **5. 在地知識是另一種科學，跨學科是另一種深度。沒有任何學科能單獨解決真實世界問題。**  
> **5. Local knowledge is another kind of science; interdisciplinarity is another kind of depth. No single discipline can solve real-world problems alone.**
